NARA Presidential Records roster

Saviorjoshh was identified in the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) presidential roster as the 52nd President of the nUSA roleplay government. NARA's August 2024 monthly update retrospectively described his July presidency as six days long, the fourth-shortest in its list. A separately maintained President chronology gives his dates as July 16–22, 2024; the date range also spans six days, but the primary records reviewed do not contain a dated oath or transfer document confirming both endpoints.[2]

Presidency

A compiled list of presidential departures dates this term from July 16 through July 22, 2024, and says Saviorjoshh finished GeorgeGodsent's term. A separate compiled vice-presidential chronology dates his service under GeorgeGodsent from April 24 through July 17, creating a two-date overlap with the presidential term. A NARA statement on July 22 confirms that a transfer was underway but does not resolve the overlap or provide oath records. The compiled documents' plaintext copies show no author, publication date, or revision history.[3]

NARA's August 2024 summary places the presidency in July and lists the resignation of GeorgeGodsent, the short tenure of Saviorjoshh, and the inauguration of Teasoups among that month's events. A compiled vice-presidential chronology lists Saviorjoshh as GeorgeGodsent's Vice President from April 24 to July 17; the compiled presidential chronology dates Saviorjoshh's term from July 16 to July 22. Those entries overlap by two calendar dates and are not reconciled by the primary archive. A July 22 NARA statement described an ongoing transition and said the next inauguration was planned for that weekend, supporting the broad timing but not the exact oath dates.[4]

The NARA monthly update ranked Saviorjoshh's six-day presidency fourth among the shortest presidencies in its table, following Smallchomama1234, Guggen_Pierre, and BernardCaldwell. It listed Dralian next, at nine days. The post labels Saviorjoshh “52st,” an apparent ordinal typo; the roster card gives “52nd.”[5]

Executive orders

The NARA Presidential Records board preserves three orders under Saviorjoshh's name. Executive Order 52-001, “To Establish Limitations on Blue-on-Blue,” revoked Executive Order 50-001 of March 12, 2024, and set disciplinary rules for one United States officer arresting another while both were on a law-enforcement team. It authorized suspension of up to ten days or termination, assigned investigations to the FBI's Criminal Investigation Division, and provided exceptions for an officer declared “rogue” or reasonably believed to pose an immediate threat to American lives.[6]

Executive Order 52-002 established a Homeland Security Council within the White House, with up to 20 presidential appointees. Its stated advisory function was to address homeland-security matters and improve communication among agency heads and law-enforcement executives; the order named several agency and force leaders for membership.[7]

Executive Order 52-003 required weekly Cabinet reports to the President and White House Chief of Staff. It directed that public versions be released 48 hours after submission with classified information removed, and that relevant unsanitized reports be sent to the congressional Gang of Eight within 24 hours. The order exempted the Chief of Staff, Vice President, and Director of National Intelligence unless separately required by written policy or presidential order.[8]

The three attached texts identify the orders by number and subject but do not show their signing dates in the captured copies. Accordingly, this article does not infer their issuance dates from their card creation dates.[9]

Pardon

A NARA board card preserves a presidential grant of clemency signed by Saviorjoshh on July 20, 2024. The document purports to pardon Saviorjoshh himself, unconditionally, for any offenses committed or alleged during 2024 through that date, including conduct before, during, and after his presidential tenure; it also directs the Bureau of Prisons to release him if confined. The available record establishes what the document says, not whether a court or other authority recognized or implemented it.[10]

Succession and ordinal records

NARA's August update associates Saviorjoshh's short July tenure with the later inauguration of Teasoups, and its roster separately lists Teasoups as the 53rd President. The primary sources reviewed do not supply an election certification, a presidential oath for Saviorjoshh, or a dated instrument of succession. The six-day duration is therefore attributed to NARA's retrospective monthly summary rather than reconstructed from a complete term record.[11]


2024 FEC election records

The FEC’s 31 March 2024 Senate Class II result notice listed Saviorjoshh first with 44 votes. This supports a short account of his elected Senate service before the presidency. The reviewed FEC presidential ballots and result records do not show him as a presidential candidate in the July or November 2024 contests.[12]
