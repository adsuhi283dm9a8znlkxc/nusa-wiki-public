GeorgeGodsent (also known as HHPrinceGeorge) was the 51st President of the United States and a repeated Speaker of the House. His administration ended executive-branch blacklists, commissioned work on the U.S. Code and clemency review, and prohibited executive agencies from using Cipher. He resigned the presidency in July 2024; the precise date and circumstances of his departure are uncertain.[2]

Election and term

The Spring 2024 election was scheduled for March, with inauguration planned for 21 March. An April announcement instead described his election and accession as occurring on 17 March. The precise accession date is uncertain.[3]

Executive orders

Executive Order 51-001, dated March 28, 2024, prohibited executive-branch blacklists or equivalent employment bars, revoked previously issued blacklists, and directed misconduct cases toward agency internal-affairs investigations or courts. The order presented this as a campaign promise and a change to executive-branch employment policy.[4]

An April 9 order established a U.S. Code Commission under the Executive Office of the President. Its assigned task was to review real-world U.S. Code provisions and propose a version adapted to the Roblox community; AlexJCabot and moneymack9999 were named chair and vice-chair. The proposed code was to go to the President for approval, after which the order and commission would become moot or dissolve.[5]

On May 1, the President established the Second Chance Commission to receive and review pardon and clemency requests, including possible sentence reductions. The order excluded serious offenses such as treason and bribery from ordinary review unless there was reasonable suspicion of a corrupt trial or procedural problem. It assigned the White House Counsel and Pardon Attorney leadership roles and described a process in which the commission would advise the President, who retained the decision on clemency.[6]

An order dated July 7, 2024, prohibited federal executive departments and agencies from using the private Cipher service, including its bot and website. It directed the Intelligence Community to develop government-owned background-check systems and said agency leaders and directors violating the order could be terminated. The Trello archive indexes the measure as “51-004 | Cipher Ban”; the attached instrument is headed “Executive Order … To Ban the Use of Cipher” and labels itself “Executive Order 51-6.”[7]

Resignation and succession

GeorgeGodsent resigned during July 2024. NARA’s August monthly summary associated that month with Saviorjoshh’s brief presidency and Teasoups’s inauguration. The precise date and circumstances of GeorgeGodsent’s departure are uncertain.[8]

House speakership

GeorgeGodsent held the speakership several times before his presidency, also using the name HHPrinceGeorge. As the newly elected Speaker, he reported House passage of the Executive Branch Blacklist Act and his Transparent Elections Bill on 23 April 2020. On 4 May, he announced Disn_Ize’s election to succeed him.[9][10]

He returned to the chair in July 2021 and introduced the “Congress Hears You” campaign to improve public participation. He appointed CottonRockefeller Speaker pro tempore. His resignation was announced on 16 August and came into effect on 17 August.[11][12]

He returned as Speaker on 17 December 2023. His program restored House committees covering judiciary and oversight, homeland security and armed services, and intelligence and foreign affairs. On 1 January 2024, he proposed an impeachment inquiry into President Akacoolguy96GPT; the proposal itself did not establish the allegations as proven. His speakership ended in February 2024.[13][14][15][16]

2024 FEC election records

In March 2024, the FEC certified GeorgeGodsent and Robbiebeastpro90000’s ticket as the winner of the Spring presidential election, reporting 330 votes for the pair. In July, the official ballot identified GeorgeGodsent as the Plutocratic Party presidential nominee; the certified results placed him second with 379 votes, behind Teasoups with 388.[17]

On 25 June 2024, the FEC said the GeorgeGodsent campaign had received a warning over “predatory campaigning,” which the notice described as dismissing non-civil-office employees who supported rival candidates. This is the FEC’s account of its warning, not an independently adjudicated finding.[18]
