DevGralius

  • Page
  • Discussion
Appearance
From nUSA Wiki

DevGralius is an account whose archived nUSA work included election communications, Clan Management, and technical and archival responsibilities. A March 2020 retirement announcement in the group's announcements channel linked a letter describing the account's departing posts and a transfer of archival custody to the Archivist of the United States.[1]

Election and management work

The archived correspondence shows election-related responsibilities before the retirement. In November 2018, an announcement said election and other-affairs questions should be sent to DevGralius while its author was away; in June 2019, DevGralius posted an extension to an election deadline after an early closure error. The messages document operational communications but do not, by themselves, establish a full term in a particular election office.[2]

In June 2019, DevGralius also announced that President-elect SpeakerJosh had released applications for the next administration, linking the announcement to the application document. Together, the election-time update and application notice show a practical communications role around election periods, while the surviving messages do not establish that DevGralius held the National Elections Administrator title during those episodes.[3]

In September 2019, a Clan Management announcement said DevGralius had resigned as Clan Manager and Discord Moderator in order to run for office in the next term. In March 2020, the retirement letter described work across Web Services, Public Relations, Legal Counsel, Development, and engineering, as well as service as Reporter of Decisions for the United States Supreme Court.[4]

The retirement letter's catalogue shows a career spanning both public-facing services and in-world institutions. It described an end to Clan Manager responsibilities in Web Services, Public Relations, Legal Counsel, and Development, plus a departure from the Chief Web Engineer role. It also recounted work as Reporter of Decisions for the Supreme Court and assignments involving District and Circuit Courts, and referred to maintaining the Judiciary Bot. The letter is a retrospective self-description of work rather than a complete staff chronology, so the article does not infer exact start or end dates for those individual posts.[5]

Archive custody transfer

The March 2020 letter said the archives had been held under the author's Clan Manager role and were being relinquished from Clan Management control. It left the National Archives with LargeTitanic2, transferred custody of the official Constitution version the author had maintained, and asked that links be updated on government and court Trello boards. The letter also designated future Archivists “Protectors of the Constitution,” reflecting the author's proposed archival stewardship model.[6]

The custody language separated archives from Group Management control while retaining a route for a future change: the letter said a later Archivist could ask pauljkl or Squareheaddude to bring the Archives back under that structure. Its requested link updates named Congress, the District Court, Court of Appeals, Supreme Court, and the Executive Office of the President Trellos, indicating that the transfer included cross-institutional reference maintenance as well as the Constitution file itself.[7]
