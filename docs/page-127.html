President of the United States

From nUSA Wiki

The President of the United States is the chief executive of the United States. The President appoints executive officials, issues executive orders, signs or vetoes legislation, and represents the country in foreign affairs. Presidents enter office through election or succession from the vice presidency. The office's authority has developed under successive constitutions and is limited by Congress, the judiciary, and Group Management.[1]

The presidential succession is numbered through the 59th presidency, held by jsjsjbsd. Separate presidencies of the same person receive separate numbers; BernardCaldwell, Dralian, Bakedgoods, TacticalRock101, and teasoups have held office more than once. The numbering contains an unresolved duplication: both teasoups and Rathuzen are designated the 54th President.[2]

Origins and the first administrations

The United States was founded by Nightgaladeld on 26 November 2012, and Nightgaladeld became its first President. From the beginning, the presidency existed alongside the separate authority of ownership and management. In December 2017, ownership transferred to pauljkl. Presidential leadership and control of the country's governing infrastructure therefore remained distinct sources of authority.[3]

The early succession passed from Nightgaladeld and Moolah60 through Marinio, three separate presidencies of Lumidas, and two of Zeyad567. SuddenRush12G served two consecutive terms in 2016, reorganizing federal agencies and security functions through executive orders. TimGeithner, the 19th President, developed recordkeeping systems and repeatedly revised the intelligence establishment during his 2018 term. Agency organization and administrative reform became recurring concerns of the early presidency.[4]

By 2017, the presidency formed part of an active political system encompassing campaigns, Congress, law-enforcement agencies, and political parties. BUDDHA encouraged participation in parties, including Isocracy and the Republicans. His use of the presidential title does not align with the later numbered succession, leaving his position in the chronology disputed.[5]

Elections, terms, and succession

Presidential elections are administered by the Federal Election Commission (FEC) and its National Elections Administrator. The Fall 2019 election plan specified a four-month term, from 21 November 2019 to 21 March 2020. The FEC certified JamesMontagu as the winner, but an eligibility injunction prevented him from qualifying immediately. His inauguration proceeded in November. The episode illustrated the distinction between winning an election, qualifying for office, and assuming the presidency.[6]

Vice-presidential succession has repeatedly maintained executive leadership between elections. Smallchomama1234 was sworn in for a brief interim period on 21 July 2019 after Bakedgoods resigned and before Josh's expected oath. Technozo, elected Vice President in 2020, later became President. Subsequent holders of the presidency included former vice presidents Lacryma, LordDeathTheReaper, Rathuzen, and snowbleed. The circumstances and precise dates of some transitions remain uncertain.[7]

The presidency has operated under several party systems. Republican, Democratic, and Constitutionalist tickets contested elections between 2018 and 2021. After periods of restrictions on party activity, an official-party system established in 2024 recognized Forward, Pioneer, and Plutocratic. By October, only Pioneer and Forward retained official status. These changes altered the organizations through which candidates campaigned and presidents built political support.[8]

Executive authority in practice

Appointments, staffing rules, and executive orders give presidents substantial influence over the operation of the federal government. Dralian introduced executive-employee participation measures and a petition system in 2019. SpeakerJosh issued orders concerning executive blacklists and official communications. JamesMontagu established the Bureau of Internal Affairs and imposed a temporary hiring freeze. Through these measures, presidents directed personnel policy, internal oversight, and communication across executive agencies.[9]

Executive orders have also addressed emergency powers and the machinery of government. Reset4K issued a broad series of administrative measures in 2020. During Bakedgoods's second presidency in 2022, an order created a Federal Combatant Coordinator and invoked temporary martial law during an invasion. In 2024, GeorgeGodsent ended executive-branch blacklists, established commissions to review legal codes and clemency requests, and prohibited agency use of the Cipher service. teasoups subsequently established a Federal Register and reporting procedures. Successive administrations thus reshaped the executive branch through both emergency measures and administrative reform.[10]

Legislation and diplomacy are central parts of presidential activity. JamesMontagu exercised both the veto and the power to sign legislation, including a veterans' observance act. SpeakerJosh and Congress issued statements during conflicts with Russia in 2019. Such episodes placed the President at the center of legislative disputes and the public presentation of foreign policy.[11]

Constitutional and management limits

The constitutional basis of presidential authority has changed repeatedly. The 2017 Constitution was superseded; a new framework followed the legal reset of 2023, another Constitution was announced as passed in 2024, and an earlier text was readopted in 2025. These changes make constitutional powers and restrictions dependent on the period in which a President served. Under the 2023 framework, the National Archives and Records Administration had public-record duties but remained under Group Management oversight.[12]

Group Management has exercised authority over the legal and political framework within which presidents govern. In July 2023, it voided existing federal laws, judicial precedent, and congressional resolutions, followed by a new Constitution and archival directives. In 2024, it ordered political parties disbanded after a referendum, then selected new official parties through MultiBallot. Presidential authority has therefore been shaped by management intervention as well as constitutional revision, legislation, and judicial decisions.[13]

Conflict, removal, and continuity

Impeachment, resignation, eligibility disputes, and management intervention have each interrupted or threatened presidential tenure. The House voted to impeach BernardCaldwell in July 2018 over allegations involving appointments and conduct; a final Senate conviction is not established. Lordsights faced impeachment proceedings and later announced his resignation. JamesMontagu encountered an eligibility injunction following the 2019 election and a management blacklist in January 2020. The blacklist was reversed after a hearing, allowing him to serve the remainder of his term.[14]

In February 2024, Group Management removed Akacoolguy96 and imposed a one-month blacklist; Robbiebeastpro90000 was then named acting President. GeorgeGodsent resigned later in 2024, although the exact date and reason remain uncertain. In 2026, two resolutions proposing the impeachment of bathamza failed. Their allegations did not result in an impeachment conviction.[15]

The constitutional crisis of May 2025 disrupted the impeachment proceedings against Rathuzen. Ownership stated that the Chief Justice had been expelled during the trial, two other justices had been blacklisted, and a remaining justice had refused to certify the impeachment. The question was referred to a District Court panel, and Rathuzen remained President pending its decision. This interim arrangement preserved his tenure while the validity of the proceedings remained unresolved.[16]

Alongside contested changes of government, scheduled inaugurations in 2020, 2021, and 2025 provided a regular means of transferring executive authority. Vice-presidential succession, election administration, and judicial proceedings offered different mechanisms for maintaining continuity. NARA's presidential collections and the later Public Law Database supported the preservation of executive and legislative acts across administrations.[17]

Presidential transitions in 2025

The presidential transitions of 2025 combined scheduled elections with an unresolved impeachment dispute. The FEC declared Rathuzen and snowbleed President-elect and Vice President-elect in March, and they were publicly identified as President and Vice President on 23 March. Rathuzen remained in office during the impeachment dispute in May. In July, the FEC certified aConArtist as the 56th President, and an inaugural notice paired him with Vice President-elect TacticalRock101. The later numbered succession instead places snowbleed 55th, Mallsoft 56th, and aConArtist 57th; the discrepancy and the date of Mallsoft's accession remain unresolved. Bathamza was certified as the 58th President-elect in November and was publicly reported sworn in on 22 November alongside Vice President aConArtist.[18]


The 2026 presidential transition

The Spring 2026 presidential election produced conflicting results. The scheduled term ran from 21 March to 21 July. On 14 March, the FEC certified Warlockings and vice-presidential candidate Epicwarrior_99, reporting 98 votes for Warlockings and 90 for Bathamza. An internal review published by American_Online on 16 March reported that a blind re-filter changed the totals to 117 for Bathamza and 109 for Warlockings. The correction notice listed changes to voter status without a candidate tally, leaving the certifications and reported recount unreconciled. On 8 June, Bathamza issued a resignation statement naming jsjsjbsd as the leader of the next administration. Contemporary news reports stated that Bathamza's election to the Senate vacated the presidency and that Vice President jsjsjbsd succeeded him, with an oath still pending.[19][20]

The FEC certified jsjsjbsd as President-elect on 16 July, reporting 131 votes for him in the Summer 2026 election. It announced 297 ballots cast and 254 valid, although the three published ticket totals added to 250. The next term was scheduled for 21 July–21 November. Because jsjsjbsd's ticket had appeared on the ballot without a vice-presidential candidate, a 22 July notice called for a vice-presidential nomination. The date of his inauguration for the new term and the outcome of that nomination remain unconfirmed. On 14 September, the FEC opened campaigning for the November election, with registration and voting details to follow.[21]
