AustinBoBoston1 is a Roblox nUSA community archivist whose documented work covered judicial records and later leadership of the National Archives and Records Administration (nUSA) (NARA). On 9 March 2024, an appointment announcement elevated Austin from Assistant Archivist for the Judicial Branch to Archivist of the United States. It said Austin had been involved with NARA since 2022 and had preserved judicial records after first serving as a District Judge.[1]

Judicial records and appointment

The March 2024 announcement described Austin’s earlier archival portfolio as judicial records and noted that the appointment allowed continued service as an Associate Justice. The stated basis for the exception was that both roles required political abstention. This was a contemporaneous appointment description; the export does not provide a full record of Austin’s judicial or archival start and end dates.[2]

NARA’s monthly update for July 2024 lists one law, four executive orders, and six Supreme Court opinions among the records summarized. It signs off with Austin as Archivist of the United States and cherrydearest as Deputy Archivist. A September staff spotlight separately called Austin a NARA Archivist and FEC Legal Commissioner and credited them with development-team quality assurance and work on an inauguration update.[3]

Records work and leadership transition

In August 2024, NARA advertised an Executive Archivist (Assistant Archivist) opening whose tasks included recording executive orders, statements, appointment dates, and nominations, liaising with the cabinet, and managing a records Trello. The post directed candidates to Austin or Dearest with questions, indicating Austin’s involvement in NARA staffing at that time. It does not assign Austin every executive-record task in the posting.[4]

A later verified nUSA announcement selected snowbleed as the next Archivist in February 2025. The announcement names the next officeholder but does not say when Austin’s service ended or describe the handoff, so the exact length of Austin’s tenure remains unspecified in the available records.[5]
