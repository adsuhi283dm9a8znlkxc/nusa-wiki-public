NUSA (Roblox group)

  • Page
  • Discussion

From nUSA Wiki

nUSA is a Roblox-based political roleplay community whose records use the name United States of America and stage federal institutions, elections, legislation and court proceedings. Its constitutions, agencies and statutes describe rules for that community's roleplay; they are not instruments of the real United States government. The surviving record reflects successive organizational resets and constitutional versions rather than one uninterrupted legal system.[1]

Scope and records

The archive documents an in-game federal government with legislative, executive and judicial records, and a separate Group Management layer. A July 2023 Group Management announcement introduced a Constitution, directives on NARA and legislative archives, and new Trello boards for NARA and Congress. Those records show the roleplay's institutional framework at that time; they do not establish the same offices or rules as current real-world U.S. institutions.[2]

The National Archives and Records Administration (nUSA) is one of the recordkeeping institutions named in the 2023 framework. The contemporary Constitution assigned it public-record storage and maintenance for the roleplay's Congress, Judiciary and Executive, while Memorandum #001 described operational autonomy from the player government under Group Management oversight. That arrangement is specific to the 2023 framework and should not be projected automatically onto later constitutions.[3]

Institutional chronology

An archival custody letter dated 13 March 2020 says the Archives and the official Constitution copy were transferred from Clan Management control to Archivist LargeTitanic2. The document is evidence of a custody handoff, but does not establish that every later version of NARA had the same organization or authority.[4]

In July 2023, Group Management announced a reset of federal laws, case law and congressional resolutions, motions and awards, effective 15 July, followed by a new Constitution and directives on 17 July. In September it credited goleros with leading NARA and the Federal Elections Commission; an October appointment assigned TacticalRummy responsibility for running NARA alongside election-administration duties. The overlap documents portfolios held by individuals, not a merger of the institutions.[5]

The 6 November 2024 announcement said a new Constitution had passed after review by the Senate, House, District Courts and Supreme Court, and that Group Management would own and maintain its PDF. The linked file was not accessible in the reviewed archive. In May 2025, NARA announced a readoption of a Constitution associated with Timothy F. Geithner, creating a later constitutional status marker; the source record does not reconcile all version dates.[6]

Constitutional sequence

The NARA Hub and Public Law Database preserve several differently dated constitutional records. These include a text marked superseded in 2017, the Constitution instituted in July 2023, a Constitution announced as passed in November 2024, and a document presented as a 2018 Constitution readopted in May 2025. A separate Public Law Database card gives the same general later readoption a 2017 origin and a January 2024 date. See Constitutions of nUSA, 2024 Constitution of nUSA and 2025 constitutional readoption in nUSA for the source-by-source account.[7]
