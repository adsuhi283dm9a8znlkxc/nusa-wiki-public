NARA Presidential Records roster

N_ightmarre was recorded on the NARA Presidential Records board as the 37th President of the nUSA roleplay community. The roster places the presidency after Dralian’s 36th term and before Ralphiuss’s 38th. An undated compiled presidential chronology reports a term from February 7 to March 21, 2021; the linked vice-presidential list also records N_ightmarre’s vice presidents serving during February and March, with the last listed term ending March 21. The primary records reviewed confirm presidential activity by February 27 but do not contain a contemporaneous oath or transition record for the reported start date. Three executive orders attributed to the administration address homeland-security coordination, internal-affairs oversight, and off-team robbery by executive-branch employees.[2]

Homeland security

Executive Order 37-001, signed at the White House on February 27, 2021, established a Homeland Security Council as an advisory body to the President. It created a Homeland Security Advisor and deputy advisor, assigned the advisor to chair the council and oversee domestic operating intelligence agencies alongside the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, and set weekly council meetings and reports. The order describes its intended structure and duties; the archive does not document implementation.[3]

Internal-affairs oversight

Executive Order 37-002, dated February 28, 2021, established N_ightmarre’s Freedom Initiative (NFI) as an independent executive-branch agency overseen by the Executive Office of the President. The order said the NFI followed the abolishment of the Internal Affairs Institute and would oversee internal-affairs work across executive agencies, receive complaints, investigate suspected misconduct, and review some disciplinary actions. It also set limits: the order excluded Clan Management community-guideline matters from NFI jurisdiction, capped suspensions, and provided an appeal process for investigated employees. These are provisions of the order; the available sources do not establish how often the agency used them.[4]

Other executive actions

A second order, dated March 4, 2021, allowed executive-branch employees to rob “off team” without agency discipline for robbery. It preserved the Department of Justice’s ability to bring criminal charges, and permitted agency internal-affairs offices to pursue reports about conduct other than robbery. The order’s title referred to equality among executive-branch “Kings and Queens”; the operative text framed the policy as a response to employees’ inability to afford in-game currency and alleged threats or blackmail. The record documents the rule as written, not its implementation or effects.[5]

On March 6, a presidential memorandum rescinded or revoked four earlier directives: a Reset4K memorandum concerning Calculatous and the White House, Josh Miller’s order banning NSFW content in federal communication servers, and two Jcmand executive orders concerning executive reform and executive entities. The memo does not list N_ightmarre’s three orders; although the Presidential Records export labels those three order cards “Repealed,” the export does not identify the repealing instrument or date, so their repeal cannot be attributed to this March 6 memorandum from the reviewed text.[6]

Judicial roster

The Supreme Court board lists an associate justice under the name “Justice Guggen_Pierre (N_ightmarre),” with service beginning March 8, 2021; it records Senate confirmation by a 6–0–1 vote and identifies N_ightmarre as the appointing President. The record does not explain the relationship between the presidential and judicial labels or provide an end date for the judicial service.[7]

The NARA presidential roster numbers N_ightmarre’s presidency 37th, between Dralian’s 36th and Ralphiuss’s 38th. The separate chronology reports February 7–March 21, 2021; the accompanying reasons list instead says N_ightmarre “Finished ArnieVinick’s term,” which conflicts with the ordinal sequence and the reported dates. The reasons list does not explain this conflict and is not a contemporaneous election or transition record.[8]
