NARA Presidential Records roster

Lacryma was the thirty-third President of the United States, succeeding BernardCaldwell in 2020 and followed by ArnieVinick. Compiled presidential histories date the term to 16 September–21 November; contemporary notices identify him as Vice President in July and September and document his later diplomacy and proposed military action against Hawaii.[2]

Vice presidency and succession

On 21 July 2020, the public announcement congratulated BernardCaldwell and Lacryma as the new President and Vice President. At a reconciliation event roster posted on 8 September, the executive delegation still listed BernardCaldwell as President and Lacryma as Vice President. The compiled presidency history dates Lacryma's accession to 16 September and says Lacryma finished BernardCaldwell's term, but the reviewed primary Discord and Trello material does not include an accession notice, oath, or explanation for the change. The FEC later certified ArnieVinick as the next President-elect on 11 November; its letter named LordDeathTheReaper as Vice President-elect. The compiled history dates the end of Lacryma's presidency to 21 November, when ArnieVinick's term began.[3]

The related vice-presidential history lists Lacryma as Vice President under BernardCaldwell from 21 July to 16 September 2020, then lists BruceASnyder as Vice President under Lacryma from 24 September to 17 November. That source is an undated compilation and does not explain the gap between 16 and 24 September. Discord independently confirms the Bernard–Lacryma pairing on 21 July and 8 September, but not the later dates or the BruceASnyder appointment.[4]

Legislative and judicial service

Records outside the presidency identify Lacryma as a senator and sponsor of Public Law 53-3, the Military Command Act, which granted specified military leaders permission to use the nUSA group shout for recruitment and notices. The Trello entry is catalogued as a public law and names Lacryma as its sponsor; it does not give a sponsorship date in the card text.[5]

During the 2020 presidential sequence, Trello records say Lacryma nominated AlexJCabot as Deputy Attorney General and Archivist of the United States; the cards report Senate confirmations on 28 September and 21 November, respectively. A judiciary-board profile also says Associate Justice SheldonParty (Lacryma) began a second term on 30 October 2020 after Senate confirmation. These are separate appointment and judicial records; the archive does not establish their exact relation to the presidential term beyond the dates stated in those cards.[6]

Diplomacy and the Hawaii dispute

Lacryma's public diplomacy included a September 2020 summit with Russia, China and Ghana and a November meeting with Russia's President and delegation. In the latter, he emphasized maintaining relations with the Russian Federation through the approaching change of administration.[7]

The closing days of his presidency also brought a confrontation over Hawaii. On 20 November, Lacryma called for an emergency joint session to authorize military action. He presented Hawaii as American territory under hostile occupation and argued that the armed forces could secure a quick victory. He proposed former President SurpriseParty as military governor, assigning the proposed office responsibility for the imprisonment and trial of the opposing authorities.[8]

The House rejected the proposed war later that day. The call for liberation therefore did not itself place the nation in an authorized war against Hawaii. The episode shows congressional resistance at the end of the administration and the difference between a President's territorial claims, military expectations and the legislature's decision to authorize force.[9]

Sources and record limits

The NARA board also contains an empty card titled “Lacryma, 43rd,” but the number conflicts with the separate compiled presidential table, which assigns the 43rd presidency to TacticalRock101. The available cards do not explain the discrepancy, so this article treats the 33rd-presidency card as the subject and does not infer a later term from the conflicting entry.[10]

The precise circumstances of Lacryma's accession remain incompletely documented. The presidential sequence and earlier vice-presidential service are established more clearly than the timing and reasons for the transition.
