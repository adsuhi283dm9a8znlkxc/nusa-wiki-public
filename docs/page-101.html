NARA Presidential Records roster

Ralphiuss was recorded by the NARA Presidential Records board as the 38th President of the nUSA roleplay community, between N_ightmarre (37th) and Guggen_Pierre (39th). A separate, undated presidential chronology reports a term from March 21 to July 16, 2021, and the linked vice-presidential roster lists Guggen_Pierre as Vice President under President RalphFoley from March 21 to July 16. The compiled chronology provides these dates, but the reviewed primary records do not include an inauguration or transition document for Ralphiuss.[2]

Presidential record

The archived Presidential Records export contains no executive-order or memorandum card under Ralphiuss’s name. The 38th-President card is present in the Presidents list, but has no description or attached document in the export. This leaves the specific policies and actions of the term undocumented in the reviewed archive.[3]

Name in the archival record

The board’s action history says the card was renamed from “RalphFoley, 38th” to “Ralphiuss, 38th” on December 25, 2023. This is a later edit to the archival card, not evidence that the presidency occurred on that date. The compiled presidency and vice-presidency lists continue to use “RalphFoley” for the 38th presidency. Together with the 2023 card rename, these records suggest that Ralphiuss and RalphFoley refer to the same roleplay identity, though the reviewed sources do not explicitly document the alias.[4]
