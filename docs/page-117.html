NARA Presidential Records roster

bathamza (also addressed as BathamzaShia in announcements) is listed as the 58th President of the Roblox nUSA roleplay government. An official announcement reported that he was sworn in on 22 November 2025 with Vice President aConArtist. A March 2026 committee notice again called the same pair President-elect and vice-President-elect and scheduled a further inauguration for 21 March. NARA's roster later placed jsjsjbsd after bathamza as the 59th President, and Bathamza issued a resignation statement on 8 June 2026. The reviewed records do not give the exact time of the transfer or his successor's oath.[2]

Roles before the presidency

Records from several institutions associate bathamza with local and federal government before the 58th presidency. The District of Columbia council board contains ordinance-list cards headed “28th Council – Mayor bathamza” and “29th Council – Mayor bathamza.” A Senate records board lists bathamza as a Class I member and as President pro tempore, while a House board has an archived membership card. These entries support service in those offices or rosters but do not by themselves provide complete terms, election results, or a sequence among them.[3]

The congressional archive also has a passed-nominations card titled BathamzaShia to be Solicitor General. The card does not preserve a service date. In an announcement from 2025, bathamza was included among applicants accepted as probationary community moderators; that community-management role should be kept distinct from the roleplay offices. The two records document additional activities without showing how long bathamza held either position.[4]

Election, inauguration, and second announced ticket

On 17 November 2025, an inaugural-committee message congratulated bathamza as President-elect and advertised a ceremony for 22 November. The National Elections Administrator's 22 November report said bathamza had “just been sworn in” as the 58th President alongside aConArtist, and reported a peak of 123 unique players at the event. This is direct evidence of the inauguration, though the reviewed message does not give a vote tally or describe the campaign.[5]

The March 19, 2026 inaugural-committee notice again congratulated bathamza and aConArtist on an election, calling them President-elect and vice-President-elect and scheduling an oath on March 21. The archive excerpt preserves the plan but no subsequent oath report or certified result for that election. It suggests a further term, while the exact March transition cannot be independently fixed from these messages alone. The later NARA board names jsjsjbsd as the next numbered President but does not state when that succession occurred.[6]

Spring 2026 contested result

The Spring 2026 presidential election produced conflicting public result records. The FEC's 14 March certification named Forward nominee warlockings the winner with 98 votes to Pioneer nominee bathamza's 90, and named Epicwarrior_99 vice-President-elect. It reported 284 ballots cast, 188 valid and 96 invalid. On 16 March, an owner account published an internal review alleging improper access to live vote data during filtering and reported a blind re-filter with revised totals of bathamza 117 and warlockings 109. That post did not include a replacement FEC certification in the reviewed archive. Its two-page change log lists 40 voter-status corrections, but no candidate totals. The 19 March inaugural-committee notice then called bathamza and aConArtist President-elect and vice-President-elect, without resolving the discrepancy between the FEC certificate and later management account.[7]

Resignation and succession

In a White House statement dated 8 June 2026, bathamza announced that he was resigning the presidency. He described nearly eight months in office across two consecutive terms and cited outside commitments and exhaustion as reasons. The letter also recounts earlier service as a House representative, deputy mayor under AlexJCabot, and later mayor, supporting a fuller sequence of offices than the archival roster cards alone provide. It addressed jsjsjbsd as the leader of the next administration.[8]

That evening, the National Elections Administrator's Senate results post included a statement vacating the presidential seat. The War Room reported that bathamza's election to the Senate prompted the vacancy and that Vice President jsjsjbsd had assumed the presidency under the roleplay 25th Amendment, with an oath still expected. N-SPAN separately reported jsjsjbsd as acting President pending an oath. These contemporary accounts support the June transition, while the reviewed sources do not record the exact oath time.[9]

Proposed impeachment and later records

In April 2026, Representative Jace_Lol123 introduced two House resolutions alleging dereliction of duty and failure to carry out constitutional responsibilities. Their attached texts are two-page proposals, not judicial findings. The congressional board files both H. Res. 4 and H. Res. 6 under Failed Items, so their existence does not establish that the House impeached bathamza or that the allegations were proven. The reviewed archive has no Senate conviction arising from either resolution.[10]

A Supreme Court petition records a claim that President bathamza imposed an executive-branch blacklist without notice or hearing. That petition states a litigant's allegation; the card alone does not establish a court ruling or the facts asserted. A later Senate Class I membership card shows bathamza in the Senate archive, but its creation or update timestamp cannot be treated as a presidential departure date. The available presidential records also lack an order collection attributed to bathamza, limiting conclusions about his administration's policies.[11]
