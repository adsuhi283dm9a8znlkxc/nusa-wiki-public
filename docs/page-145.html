News organization in nUSA FTN News was a news organization in nUSA associated with the Federal Television Network. Led by founders GreatJakers1113 and Jonofdoom, it covered presidential elections, congressional leadership and military administration, published editorials, and collaborated with Reuters on a planned presidential debate. Jonofdoom served as News Director. On 12 October 2025, the founders announced the company's closure after approximately five years of operation.[1][2]

Origins and leadership

FTN's founders described the company as approximately five years old at its closure in October 2025, placing its origins around 2020. In their farewell, they characterized FTN as nUSA's longest-running news source.[1]

GreatJakers1113 and Jonofdoom shared leadership of the organization. Jonofdoom also wrote reporting and opinion pieces and helped create its internal code of ethics. Its publications used the corporate name Federal Television Network, Inc.; an editorial's notice also referred to FTN Media Group.[2][3]

Political reporting in 2024

FTN covered the opening of the autumn presidential campaign. On 10 October 2024, it reported campaign launches by incumbent President Teasoups and Nasanovuh, alongside a reported merger of the Plutocratic and Forward parties. Its coverage followed both the presidential contest and changes in party organization.[4]

On 10 November, after the certification of Teasoups's victory, FTN reported ApolloKorman's concession. Its campaign coverage therefore extended from candidate announcements through the contest's conclusion.[5]

FTN also reported on congressional leadership. Its 29 December article, “Speaker Arthur_Chen Resigns, KaceyMontagu Aims To Take Helm,” covered Arthur_Chen's departure amid a dispute over attempts to remove him as Speaker. The report addressed the transition in House leadership and KaceyMontagu's effort to secure the office.[6]

Military administration coverage

In January 2025, FTN published Jonofdoom's six-page report on the proposed abolition of the military Board of Governors. The report examined the competing arguments for continuity in military leadership and renewed political oversight of appointments.[3]

Jonofdoom obtained comments from Secretary of Defense FighterHath, Senator RoFiles, President Teasoups and military officers, and sought a response from Group Manager AcidRaps. FighterHath defended the board as protection against politically motivated personnel changes, while RoFiles argued that its arrangements weakened accountability. Teasoups described work on a legislative replacement. The article included an anonymous service member's criticism of the board and FighterHath's stated intention to resign if it were abolished.[3]

Editorial direction

On 13 March 2025, Jonofdoom published “In Defense of Biased News,” responding to a controversy over NBC4's coverage of presidential candidate AConArtist. He opposed restrictions on partisan publication, favoring public rebuttal and fact-checking. At the same time, he pledged impartial reporting at FTN and referred to its internal ethics code. The publication explicitly identified the piece as his opinion rather than the position of FTN's management or staff.[2]

Summer 2025 election coverage

On 26 June 2025, FTN and Reuters announced a joint presidential debate scheduled for 30 June and invited questions from the public.[7]

FTN subsequently projected a victory for AConArtist in the summer presidential election on 20 July. The announcement was an election projection issued by the newsroom.[8]

Closure

GreatJakers1113 and Jonofdoom announced FTN's closure on 12 October 2025. They explained that they could no longer devote the time needed to maintain the company. Their statement also said its DOCAL license had been revoked and that they did not intend to renew it.[1]
