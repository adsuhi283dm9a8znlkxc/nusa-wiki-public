Frosty_TheSn0wman is a Roblox account recorded in nUSA community sources as Vice President in October 2018 and President Frosty in January 2019. A poll listed Frosty among candidates for the next presidential term that October, but the archive does not establish the election result or the date he took office.[2]

Vice presidency and transition

On October 6, Frosty warned the community about an account impersonating him and identified himself as Vice President. The next day's community poll asked whom members supported for the next presidency and listed Frosty, mario and AcidRaps as candidates. The post records a poll rather than a certified election result.[3]

By January 13, 2019, the archive identified him as President Frosty and reproduced a statement attributed to his presidential account. The sources reviewed do not include his oath or specify when the transition from Vice President to President occurred.[4]

Disputed foreign policy

On January 13, after the Senate (nUSA) rejected the Treaty of Washington, Frosty posted that the time for considering peace was over and that the military stood ready to fight the United Kingdom. The archive records the Senate result and his public position; it does not independently establish the events or claims underlying the Roblox roleplay conflict.[5]

The next day, the channel linked a presidential statement about future conflict with the United Kingdom and a separate document described as a Russian ultimatum. The accompanying ultimatum document set out Russia's in-world demands, including an apology and resignations by U.S. intelligence leaders and President Frosty. These are demands made in the roleplay document, not independently verified events.[6]

On January 14, a post attributed to Senator SmallCho's Special Counsel's Office reproduced the beginning of a statement saying the office had found sufficient information to determine that the President, Chief of Staff and other officials had committed “high crimes.” The archived excerpt is cut off before describing the alleged conduct, and the reviewed messages do not establish the proceeding's final disposition. The statement is therefore treated here as the Special Counsel's claim, not a finding independently verified by the source archive.[7]

Resignation

On January 15, Frosty announced that his resignation as President would take effect at midnight. In the statement reproduced in the Discord archive, he criticized what he described as nUSA's recurring pattern of removing presidents each term. That was Frosty's explanation and assessment of his decision; the archive does not establish a causal link between his resignation and the Special Counsel statement posted the prior day.[8]

The archived messages establish the resignation date but do not identify a successor in the same announcement. The opening and closing dates of Frosty's presidency therefore remain incomplete in this source record.[9]
