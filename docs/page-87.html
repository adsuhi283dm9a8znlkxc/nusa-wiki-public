Dralian (also known as realDralian) was recorded by the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) as the 23rd president of the nUSA roleplay community. Contemporary Discord records identify him as president by January 20, 2019; he said on March 13 that he would not seek another term, and the archive announced his successor’s oath on March 21. The surviving record does not establish the exact date he first assumed office. The presidential ordinal is disputed: NARA’s roster calls Dralian the 23rd president, while the oath post by his successor, Procursive, also identifies Procursive as the 23rd.[2]

Election and transition

The January 2019 transition followed President Frosty_TheSn0wman’s announcement that his resignation would take effect at midnight on January 15. Dralian was identified as President in a January 20 notice inviting members to watch his first address to Congress. These posts place him in office by January 20, but do not record his oath or specify whether he became President immediately when Frosty’s resignation took effect.[3]

On February 18, an election announcement named Republican Dralian and independent candidate DevGralius on the ballot for the 2019 Winter Election for President. The surviving announcement establishes that Dralian was a candidate, but the reviewed election records do not include a certified result or explain the relationship between that election and the presidential transition already recorded in January.[4]

On March 13, a post attributed to Dralian thanked the community for what he described as progress over the prior two months and expressed excitement for the future. A follow-up message clarified that he was declining to seek another term, rather than resigning immediately, and said Procursive would be the next President. The archive then announced that Procursive had been sworn in on March 21. The oath post reproduced in the channel identifies Procursive as the 23rd President, creating an unresolved disagreement with NARA’s roster entry for Dralian.[5]

Administration

A surviving notice from February 2 records an unusually forceful announcement about the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA): Dralian said it “no longer exists,” directed that team members be arrested, and warned that anyone found on the team would be banned. The archived statement does not include a separate order, its rationale, or evidence that arrests or bans followed. It therefore documents what the President announced, rather than the implementation or consequences of the directive.[6]

Other posts document efforts to organize the executive branch and create public channels for engagement. On February 19, Dralian required federal employees to join an Executive Branch Discord within 48 hours or risk losing their jobs. On February 27, he announced the White House’s “We the People” petition system, saying that petitioners who reached a signature threshold were guaranteed a response. The source describes the announced rules and purpose, but does not preserve the threshold or a record of the system’s subsequent operation.[7]

The archive also records several appointments and public events. A February 2 White House post said Dralian promoted LordDeathTheReaper to Admiral of the Navy. On February 19, Vice President Bakedgoods thanked Dralian for selecting him and said he had been sworn in after Senate confirmation. Dralian announced a State of the Union address for March 2; the archive confirms the event was publicized and the address was held, but the linked speech itself is not reproduced in the reviewed record.[8]

Judicial nominations and later service

In the final days of his presidency, Dralian nominated WalterMondale (identified parenthetically as “Dralian” in a court roster) to the United States Supreme Court as an associate justice. The court’s former-member card says the Senate confirmed the nomination 9–3–1 on March 21 and that he was sworn in on March 22, the day after Procursive’s oath. The same board later listed “Justice Samuel Chase (Dralian)” as a separate associate-justice entry, but the card calls that a “Second Term”; the available records do not fully explain the name and term changes.[9]

NARA’s Presidential Records board also contains a card titled “Dralian | Executive Order #23-001,” marked “Repealed.” The captured board export contains no order text or attached PDF for that card, so its title and later label do not establish what the order did, when it was issued, or when it was repealed. The same board has a separate “Dralian, 36th” President card. A later Discord retrospective lists “Dralian (9 days)” among short presidencies, but does not specify which numbered term it refers to. The later roster entry and nine-day note are treated as a separate second presidency below; the surviving records do not provide enough detail to merge the two terms into one chronology.[10]

The reviewed sources do not establish prior service by Dralian as a senator, representative, mayor, or city council member. An earlier May 2018 Discord poll names him in a presidential debate, and the February 2019 ballot identifies him as a presidential candidate; neither notice establishes a prior elected office or the result of the earlier contest.[11]

Second presidency

The NARA Presidential Records roster lists Dralian as the 36th President, after LordDeathTheReaper (35th) and before N_ightmarre (37th). A separate, undated presidential chronology reports a term from January 29 to February 7, 2021. A linked vice-presidential roster places Dralian in that office through January 29 and Guggen_Pierre from February 4 to 7 under Dralian and N_ightmarre; an August 2024 NARA monthly update says Dralian’s presidency lasted nine days. These sources align with the reported dates, although the exports do not include a contemporaneous oath or inauguration record for this term.[12]

The reasons-for-leaving-office list labels Dralian’s 36th presidency “Blacklisted,” but it does not name the authority or provide a date or explanation. The archived Presidential Records board contains a card titled “Dralian | Executive Order #23-001,” but the export has no order text or attachment, and the order number does not connect it to the 36th presidency. The reviewed records therefore do not support attributing a particular policy or blacklisting action to this term.[13]
