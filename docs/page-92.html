SpeakerJosh (also referred to as Josh in nUSA announcements) is a Roblox account recorded as President of the nUSA roleplay community beginning in July 2019. He was identified as President-elect in June, and a transition post on July 21 said he was expected to be sworn in later that day. By July 22, Josh was issuing presidential announcements. NARA later catalogued the same-period presidency as “JoshMiller, 27th,” with July orders filed under the visually similar “JoshMiIIer” spelling. The chronology and orders strongly associate those archival names with SpeakerJosh, although the sources reviewed do not supply an explicit alias statement or account ID.[2]

Prior office

A June 3, 2019 Federal Elections Commission announcement delivered House Class I results to the “outgoing Speaker, @Josh.” Together with the later SpeakerJosh President-elect announcement and July presidential posts, this supports a prior Speaker of the House role for the same 2019 political identity. The export does not preserve a platform account ID linking every display name, so the earlier office is stated from the dated record rather than from a verified account profile.[3]

Election and transition

On June 18, 2019, an nUSA update announced that President-elect SpeakerJosh had released applications for the next administration. The attached application described a term scheduled from July 21 through November 21, 2019, and set a five-day application window. It said transition team members would score applications, allowed the team to disqualify applicants, and provided for random extra moderation. This was a published staffing procedure, not a record of who was ultimately selected for each job.[4]

On July 21, a channel post said outgoing President Bakedgoods had resigned, Vice President SmallCho had been sworn in temporarily, and President-elect Josh was expected to take office later that day. The next day, Josh's account announced Executive Order J-001. The archive establishes this handover sequence but does not supply the exact time or text of his oath in the messages cited here.[5] The campaign and handover appear in the wider record of the 2019 presidential elections (nUSA).

Executive administration

In the first weeks of the term, Josh announced a change to White House recruiting: executive-branch applications would use the White House website, with the transition proceeding gradually while question packs were prepared. The announcement said White House intern applications were already available and clarified that recruitment had not stopped. In a separate notice, the White House sought a videographer to create regular communications videos, described as an alternative to relying only on press releases.[6]

The archive records Josh announcing seven medals and an August press statement about executive recruitment. It does not preserve the criteria for the medals or identify the eventual recipients in those posts, so the notices show the actions announced rather than their later effects.[7]

Two July orders are preserved in the NARA presidential archive. J-001, dated July 22, overhauled the executive-branch blacklist process and set out an interim appeal arrangement; its text refers to the “Josh administration.” J-002 addressed removal of NSFW channels and content from executive communications servers. The J-002 PDF header says July 24 while its signature block says July 22; a July 24 Discord message records the President announcing that the order had been signed. These sources establish the orders' text and release notices, but not full enforcement or later effects.[8]

Russia conflict

During the Roblox roleplay war with Russia in July 2019, Josh posted that the United States should condemn harassment based on gender, sexuality, race, religion or opinion. A two-page public letter from July 24 framed the conflict as a dispute over United Nations Department of Political Affairs negotiations. In the letter, Josh said the United States had accepted Russia's proposed war terms and submitted parallel terms of its own, but that Russia had rejected the U.S. proposals; these are his administration's claims in the conflict.[9]

On July 27, nUSA announced a 2–0 victory over Russia and Josh posted a statement celebrating it. On August 1, Josh announced a new declaration of war with the United Kingdom against Russia/Soviet Union, and the channel reported that both houses of the United States Congress (nUSA) had passed a declaration of war. The archive therefore records two distinct stages: an announced end to the July conflict and a later campaign for a second one. These were community roleplay events, not real-world military actions.[10]

The application set a planned November 21 end date, but Josh left office earlier, on 29 August 2019.[1][11]
