Matrix oc is an American politician who served as Speaker of the House.

Office Documented tenure
Speaker of the House 18 February–April 2025

Political career

Matrix_oc, also using the name MitchMatrix, became Speaker on 18 February 2025. He called for cooperation between the Pioneer and Forward caucuses and restoration of the House’s legislative standing.[1]

His term included renewed debate over the House rules. On 26 February, the Parliamentarian advised him that rules adopted at a prior session should be invalidated and voted on again. The following day, Majority Leader Jjc0308 reported a session that reviewed twenty-one legislative items and passed new rules.[2][3]

On 15 March, he invited President Teasoups to deliver a State of the Union address the following evening. Technozo became Speaker after the next election, certified on 8 April.[4][5]
