NARA Presidential Records

Marinio was the third President of the Roblox nUSA roleplay community. His administration issued a November 2013 executive order barring federal employers from requiring applicants without Builders Club membership to wear a particular outfit, and advanced a congressional measure establishing elected city mayors. He took office after the disputed end of Moolah60's presidency; he served from 26 August to 30 December 2013.[1][2]

Presidency

Marinio took office after Moolah60's disputed presidency and was succeeded by Lumidas. An earlier list of presidents omitted Moolah60, placing Marinio directly after Nightgaladeld; the presidential roster recognizes Moolah60 as the second President and Marinio as the third. The precise date of Marinio's accession is not established by a contemporary instrument.[3]

On 29 November 2013, Marinio issued Executive Order #3-001 on hiring requirements. It prohibited federal agencies from making a specific outfit mandatory for applicants who did not have Builders Club membership, describing such a rule as discrimination based on social and economic status. The order threatened employment termination, federal court proceedings, criminal records, and suspension from federal agencies for officials who enforced the requirement. The surviving copy records the order’s text and stated effective time, 12:30 p.m. Eastern; it does not document how widely agencies applied it.[4]

Congressional records also associate Marinio with a city-government measure. Public Law 6-1 proposed elected mayors responsible for local construction, security, ordinances, and civic events. The proposal set eligibility and election rules, including a minimum of 50 votes and a 10-point margin. The Public Law Database links a separate post as Marinio’s signature record; the surviving card presents the bill and signature link but does not supply a vote tally or enactment date.[5]

Later public service

A December 2016 profile of President SuddenRush12G described Marinio as the President under whom SuddenRush12G led the United States Special Forces. This is a later account of SuddenRush12G’s career and does not provide additional dates for Marinio’s presidency.[6]
