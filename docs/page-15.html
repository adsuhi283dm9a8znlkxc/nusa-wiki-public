ZhangJingping (also rendered as “Zhang Jinping” in signed announcements) is a Roblox nUSA community archivist who was selected as Archivist of the United States in March 2025. The appointment post cited Zhang’s prior work maintaining the court system and its records. Later posts document archival publications, deputy appointments, and an additional National Elections Administrator (NEA) title; they do not supply a complete succession timeline or define the duration of each appointment.[1]

Appointment and archival work

The March 2025 announcement described Zhang as the sole custodian of the judiciary’s records after maintaining the court system for more than a year, and selected Zhang to lead NARA. It framed the appointment as part of an effort to streamline the agency and preserve nUSA history during preparation for a new gameplay experience. Those statements document the appointing announcement’s rationale, rather than an independent evaluation of the court records or a complete account of their custody.[2]

On 28 April, Zhang posted a Deputy Archivist vacancy and described the position as supporting national records management. The posting required political abstention, specifically prohibiting campaigning, and requested an application including a résumé and references. This provides evidence of the role’s stated expectations during Zhang’s leadership.[3]

In May 2025, Zhang signed a post announcing that NARA had prepared an accessible copy of the Constitution of the United States for use, including revisions enacted through a cited public law and Senate joint resolution. This was an archival publication and access measure; it does not by itself establish the full content or custody chain of the underlying constitution.[4]

Deputy appointments and election administration

In July 2025, Zhang announced bones as the new Deputy Archivist. In February 2026, a further post announced v0idedghost as “the next Deputy Archivist.” The source does not explain how the later selection related to bones’s appointment, so these are best treated as dated appointments rather than proof of uninterrupted or exclusive succession.[5]

A staffing post dated 10 April 2026 announced gabezilla090909 as Deputy National Elections Administrator after 23 applications. Zhang signed that notice with both the Archivist of the United States and National Elections Administrator titles. This documents the two titles appearing together in that announcement; it does not establish that NARA and the FEC were a single agency or that Zhang held the NEA role throughout the preceding archival period.[6]
