NARA Presidential Records

TheySinned was the 13th President of the Roblox nUSA roleplay community. He served from 21 January to 25 February 2017, succeeding SuddenRush12G and preceding Rapidaax. Before his election, he worked in federal security and intelligence roles, served in the Senate, and became known for sponsoring the impeachment of former President Zeyad567. His brief presidency addressed employee conduct, executive staffing and planning for confrontation with Britain.[2]

Career and election

TheySinned worked in the Department of Homeland Security and its intelligence office before entering national politics. During SuddenRush12G's second administration, he served as deputy secretary of Homeland Security and later as a senator. In Congress, he submitted articles of impeachment against Zeyad567. His presidential campaign promised broader access to executive employment, training programs, job creation, and a less restrictive approach to small business and development.[3]

TheySinned defeated former President Jcmand in the winter 2016–17 election. The contest became a debate over continuity and change: Jcmand drew support from established political figures, while TheySinned presented himself as a challenger to the political order. Timothy Geithner, a former Vice President and senior official who had worked with Jcmand, publicly endorsed TheySinned and described the campaign's emphasis on working citizens, employment, and political participation. As a campaign endorsement, Geithner's account records one contemporary interpretation of the race rather than an official election tally.[4]

British confrontation and war planning

The new administration confronted renewed conflict with Britain. In an address dated 28 January, TheySinned said that Congress had overridden SuddenRush12G's veto of the Sovereignty Act on 22 January, undoing an earlier peace arrangement negotiated by SuddenRush, TimGeithner and Lumidas. He reported a British declaration of war and coalition preparations, criticized the congressional decision, and announced higher military readiness, intelligence restrictions and negotiations over war terms. These were the President's explanation of the crisis and his allocation of responsibility for it.[5]

A separate executive order, dated 22 January and published the following day, created a War Commission comprising the President, Vice President, senior security and diplomatic officials, and designated military commanders. Legislative leaders and other officials held advisory places. The commission was assigned military planning, intelligence coordination, deployment advice and recommendations to Congress; the order also described the Vice President as co-commander-in-chief. The war preparations demonstrate that TheySinned's brief presidency extended beyond domestic employment policy. A final settlement of this January confrontation has not been established by the surviving records reviewed here.[6]

Presidency

The employee-conduct order of 30 January targeted abusive conduct in federal employment. It defined an abusive employee to include a current or former federal employee found to have engaged in conduct considered unsuitable for the executive branch, or someone declared “rogue” by a federal judge or Supreme Court justice. The order directed that such employees be placed on an executive blacklist, made the blacklist public, and required executive employees to report substantiated abuse to the Internal Affairs Department. It also exposed officials who knowingly hired blacklisted personnel to dismissal.[7]

A second order, issued on 20 February, limited most executive employees to positions in two departments or agencies. It set out exceptions for employees representing a separate executive entity, military personnel, and divisions operating solely within one parent agency. Department and agency heads were required to review staffing and dismiss employees who remained in violation after the transition period. The order addressed the administration's concern that officials held multiple positions without carrying out the duties of each.[8]

His administration also made appointments in the intelligence community. In January, TheySinned nominated LargeTitanic2 for director of national intelligence, following his service in Homeland Security's Office of Intelligence and Analysis. The Senate confirmed the nomination, and LargeTitanic2 thanked the President in a public statement after confirmation. TheySinned also appointed British2004 acting director of the National Security Agency while the Senate considered the appointment.[9]

Resignation

TheySinned resigned on 25 February 2017, after 35 days in office, for personal reasons.[10] Vice President PurgeSabre also left office, and Rapidaax succeeded him. The Homeland Security Department subsequently described the resignations of the President, Vice President, and White House chief of staff as a disruption to department operations. The Presidential Records board preserves two employment orders; a separate forum order documents the War Commission and should not be conflated with the employee-conduct order also numbered #1.[11]
