Guggen_Pierre, also styled GuggenPierre, was the 39th President of the United States, serving from 16 to 21 July 2021. Previously vice president under RalphFoley, he succeeded him following his resignation and completed the remaining days of the presidential term before LeoVinick's scheduled inauguration. His five-day presidency included judicial appointments and a series of executive nominations.[1][2]

Vice presidency

The Federal Election Commission certified the Winter 2021 election on 14 March. Its results letter declared RalphFoley the winning Constitutionalist presidential candidate and Guggen_Pierre vice president-elect. The Commission reported 444 ballots received and 111 disqualified for alternate accounts or lack of citizenship. It also waived the requirement for the president-elect to submit a separate letter to Congress declaring his vice-presidential choice and certified Guggen directly from the campaign.[2]

The compiled vice-presidential chronology dates his service under RalphFoley from 21 March to 16 July 2021. That service preceded his accession to the presidency rather than constituting a separate presidential election victory.[3]

Presidency

Succession and the remaining term

RalphFoley announced his resignation shortly after midnight Eastern time on 16 July, thanking the country for the preceding months in office. Guggen succeeded him five days before the next administration was due to take office. The Summer 2021 election had already been certified in LeoVinick's favor on 12 July, and the FEC's term plan set the inauguration for 21 July.[4][5]

The brevity of the presidency reflected a succession near the end of an existing term. Guggen's departure coincided with the scheduled inauguration, rather than an early resignation from a new four-month administration.[1][5]

Appointments and nominations

Guggen nominated Yiddished and Kind_Yada to Supreme Court vacancies in the opening hours of his presidency. The court's records date their confirmations and swearing-in to 16 July, with Senate votes of 6–0–0 for Yiddished and 4–3–0 for Kind_Yada.[6][7]

Other opening announcements appointed r6kt as White House chief of staff and SurpriseParty to a Senate vacancy. They also nominated bobbert as vice president. The latter nomination was accompanied by criticism of congressional bureaucracy and praise for his experience in the community; it was a nomination rather than a verified confirmation.[8]

The administration announced further nominations for executive departments and agencies, including homeland security, treasury, the FBI and SWAT. Some nominations were revised almost immediately: a Secretary of Defense nomination was rescinded in favor of retaining SolarGeneral, and a transportation nomination was later changed to a deputy position. These announcements indicate active staffing changes despite the limited time remaining in the term.[9]

Activity and municipal reorganization

Guggen's presidency coincided with the implementation of a management decision to discontinue municipal government. On 18 July, Aerium announced that five municipal groups had been cleared: the Metropolitan Police Department, State Police/Nevada Highway Patrol, Washington Emergency Medical Services, Washington Fire Department and City Council. He said they could reopen after legislation incorporated them into the federal government. The change implemented an earlier management announcement, rather than a new policy originating with Guggen.[10]

Activity in Washington was uneven during this period. A White House invitation on 18 July advertised 44 players in the capital, even as municipal institutions were being reorganized. The announced count represents a particular session and does not establish the city's average activity throughout the term.[11]

Handover to LeoVinick

On 21 July, Ronnie invited citizens to the inauguration of the new administration. LeoVinick issued a signed nomination notice that morning, naming his administration's officers and declaring nominees acting officers pending Senate confirmation. Guggen's tenure ended at that scheduled transition.[12]
