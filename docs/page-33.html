2024 Constitution of nUSA

  • Page
  • Discussion
Appearance
From nUSA Wiki

The 2024 Constitution of nUSA is the constitutional text that Group Management announced as passed on 6 November 2024 after review by the roleplay Senate, House, District Courts and Supreme Court. The announcement described its two-column presentation and said Group Management would own and maintain the PDF. The linked document could not be retrieved in the reviewed source archive, so the text's substantive articles and exact effective provisions remain unverified here.[1]

Approval announcement

The announcement reported approval from four institutions: the Senate, House, District Courts and Supreme Court. It described the document as a new Constitution that “has passed” and noted a two-column format inspired by the National Constitution Center's presentation. This is a contemporaneous Group Management report of the approval sequence; no individual votes or convention record are included in the accessible announcement excerpt.[2]

Group Management was identified as the owner and maintainer of the PDF. That custody statement differs from the May 2025 NARA-linked copy's cover, which identifies the Archivist's document as a readopted version; it does not by itself settle the broader division of archival and constitutional authority between GM and NARA.[3]

Surviving status evidence

The original 2024 Constitution attachment was linked as “Constitution2024.pdf,” but the source review recorded a permission failure and did not preserve a readable copy. The NARA Hub later listed “GobiesCons 11-6-24” and marked it inactive. The available records therefore support the reported passage and later inactive label, but do not support paraphrasing specific rights, branches, terms or amendment rules in the missing text.[4]

In May 2025, NARA announced the readoption of a Constitution associated with Timothy F. Geithner, and the Hub's TimCons card identifies its copy as re-adopted on 12 May 2025. This later record does not describe the 2024 text's provisions or fully explain whether the 2024 version was repealed, superseded or otherwise displaced; the Hub's inactive label is the clearest surviving status marker.[5]

Context

The Constitution announced in 2024 followed the July 2023 Constitution, which had been published and instituted after Group Management's legal reset. The 2023 copy explicitly defines NARA's records role; because the 2024 copy is unavailable, that mandate cannot be assumed to have persisted unchanged into the 2024 text.[6]

This article concerns the constitution of the nUSA roleplay on Roblox. It does not refer to the Constitution of the real United States. For the broader sequence and conflicting version dates, see Constitutions of nUSA and nUSA (Roblox group).[7]
