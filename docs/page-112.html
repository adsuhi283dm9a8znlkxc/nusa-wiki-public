NARA Presidential Records roster

Rathuzen is listed by the National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) as the 54th President of the nUSA roleplay government. He was announced as President-elect Teasoups's Vice President-elect in November 2024 and was publicly inaugurated as President on March 23, 2025. NARA's roster also records him as the 64th Vice President, while its list contains another President card labeled “54th” for Teasoups; the surviving records do not explain the duplicated presidential ordinal or the vice-presidential numbering.[2]

Vice presidency and inauguration

An announcement on November 10, 2024, said President Teasoups and Vice President-elect Rathuzen would be sworn in on November 23. Teasoups's Executive Order 008, issued on November 23, identified the date as the start of that administration's second term. NARA's roster lists Rathuzen separately as the 64th Vice President, but the available announcement and roster card do not include the oath text or explain the ordinal.[3]

The owner-affiliated announcements archive publicized Rathuzen's presidential inauguration on March 23, 2025, and another same-day post congratulated “President of the United States Rathuzen.” A following post identified snowbleed as Vice President. NARA's roster lists Rathuzen as the 54th President. The reviewed records do not provide a certified result or a primary document describing the mechanism by which he became President.[4]

Executive orders

The NARA Presidential Records board preserves five Rathuzen orders under card identifiers 55-001 through 55-005. Their attached texts use Federal Register executive-order numbers 012 through 016, a difference in numbering the archive does not explain.[5]

Executive Order 012, “Fair Recruitment System,” dated March 25, 2025, directed agencies to use hiring standards that did not require prior experience for lower-ranking or non-terminating positions. It encouraged transparent criteria, feedback, and appeals and prohibited discrimination on listed protected grounds. It assigned the Department of Commerce and Labor responsibility for collecting employment procedures and establishing an appeals system.[6]

Executive Order 013, dated April 21, directed the Department of Homeland Security to audit federal law-enforcement agencies. The prescribed primary audits covered events, promotions, internal affairs, activity, and forward plans; optional secondary audits could cover policy changes, joint events, and training. Findings were to be sent to DHS and the White House, with public release left to the discretion of the President and DHS Secretary.[7]

Three orders dated May 12 addressed law-enforcement conduct and employment. Executive Order 014 prohibited “door cuffing,” “cuff rushing,” and taser spamming, while permitting door blocking only during active protective or security details by specified protective agencies; it provided for suspension or termination for violations. Executive Order 015 restricted on-duty political discussion, advocacy, and endorsements by federal officers and prohibited using federal employment for political advantage. Executive Order 016 required federal agencies to verify that new hires held at least the group rank of “American Citizen”; people still undergoing immigration were not to be hired until verified. The orders assigned reporting and enforcement functions to the Department of Homeland Security's investigations office, with agency-level discipline described in the texts.[8]

Impeachment and constitutional dispute

In a May 20, 2025, message to the community, the owner said the President's impeachment trial had been disrupted when the Chief Justice was expelled and two Associate Justices were blacklisted, and that Justice RummyLeroux had refused to certify the impeachment. The owner described the situation as a constitutional crisis and referred the dispute to a three-judge District Court panel, stating that Rathuzen would remain President unless the court decided otherwise. On 23 May, the panel held that the trial's presiding-justice succession was defective, ordered a new Senate vote, and stayed its order until a Chief Justice or eligible acting successor could be seated. The opinion did not decide the underlying allegations.[9]

On May 26, an owner-affiliated announcement said an attached formal letter addressed events “concerning the 25th Amendment,” actions by the Vice President and Cabinet, and the legal standing of President Rathuzen. The archived announcement does not reproduce the letter's contents; it establishes that the matter remained a public subject of dispute, but not the letter's conclusions or the eventual outcome.[10]

Presidential ordinal

NARA's Presidential Records board contains both “Rathuzen, 54th” and “Teasoups, 54th.” Teasoups's November 23, 2024, Executive Order 008 calls itself the beginning of that administration's second term, and its attached text was signed by Teasoups as President; the March 2025 announcements identify Rathuzen as the incoming President. The sources reviewed do not state whether the duplicate 54th is an error or reflects the archive's counting method. This article therefore uses NARA's labels as roster entries, without treating the ordinal as a settled chronology.[11]


2025 FEC election records

The FEC's March 2025 ballot listed Rathuzen and snowbleed as the Pioneer Party ticket. Its 16 March results notice reported 60.75% for the pair and declared them President-elect and Vice President-elect, with 23 March as the scheduled start of the term. On 23 March, an owner-affiliated post announced that Rathuzen's inauguration was happening, and later posts identified him as President and snowbleed as Vice President. These records support the announced transition but do not include the oath text. On 20 May, ownership stated that Rathuzen remained President during the impeachment dispute; the District Court's 23 May opinion later ordered a fresh Senate vote while staying the order until a lawfully seated presiding justice was available.[12]
