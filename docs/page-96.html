ArnieVinick was the 34th President of the United States, serving from 21 November 2020 to 9 January 2021. Elected as the Republican candidate with LordDeathTheReaper as his running mate, he introduced employment and military-service initiatives and reorganized parts of the executive branch. He resigned after admitting that he had initiated a proposed bribery arrangement, while also describing the burdens of the presidency and worsening political hostility.[1][2][3]

Election and transition

The FEC's certified results letter reported 831 votes received, with 341 manually disqualified for alternate accounts or lack of nUSA citizenship. It named Arnie as the Republican Party's winning candidate and LordDeathTheReaper as Vice President-elect. The FEC post releasing the result appeared in #announcements on 10 November and linked the signed results letter dated 11 November. Arnie was inaugurated on 21 November, as confirmed by the contemporary invitation and oath photograph.[4]

Executive orders

Arnie's 21 November 2020 order sought to reduce combat tryouts for executive-branch jobs. It barred departments and agencies from using combat tryouts as a hiring standard, allowed presidential exemptions, and listed Special Forces and the Special Weapons and Tactics Team as initial exemptions; the United States Military was permanently exempted. The order's stated rationale was to emphasize other skills for most executive jobs and make combat tryouts less common.[5]

On 22 December 2020, Arnie ordered the Special Weapons and Tactics Team redirected under the Department of Justice, with its commander reporting to the Attorney General. The order said the team would retain its authority to operate and enforce law. A separate Trello card records that Arnie revoked BernardCaldwell's Executive Order 32-003 establishing the Internal Affairs Institute; the card says no formal order was written, and its attached screenshot is a 23 November 2020 announcement. The screenshot documents the announcement and the stated revocation, not a surviving signed instrument.[6]

Two orders dated 5 January 2021 created employment programs linked to military service. One established a Veterans' Assistance Program jointly managed by the Departments of Veterans Affairs and Commerce and Labor, with a database of veterans' employment status and active-duty personnel and a process for participating agencies to consider eligible veterans for jobs. The other directed listed federal agencies to offer promotions or promotional points to eligible employees after at least 30 days of honorable military service, subject to agency rules and exclusions for capped senior positions. The documents establish what the orders directed; the archive does not show how fully the programs were implemented.[7]

Resignation

On 9 January 2021, Arnie submitted a signed resignation to Secretary of State ThomasLaRusso, effective at 5:00 p.m. Eastern Standard Time. His accompanying farewell admitted initiating a proposed bribe involving a longtime friend and accepted responsibility. He apologized to the officials who had served honestly in his administration and said his conduct had undermined their work.[1][2]

The farewell also described personal and political strain. Arnie said that the presidency had ceased to be enjoyable, that he had lost friends and that its responsibilities had been greater than anticipated. He regretted leaving much of his campaign agenda unfinished, while praising his appointees and the Veterans' Assistance Program, which he described as still being developed. He expressed confidence in LordDeathTheReaper's succession and urged citizens to remain engaged and hold their leaders accountable.[2]

Arnie connected the country's difficulties to increasing toxicity, which he believed had driven the community away from its earlier peak. This was his assessment of the political climate at the time of his departure, rather than a measured account of player activity.[2]

Other recorded office

A judiciary-board profile identifies LewisFPowellJr (ArnieVinick) as an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court, appointed by President ArnieVinick, with an incumbency beginning 22 December 2020 and retirement on 19 April 2021. The profile does not explain how that service related to Arnie's presidential tenure, so the dates are reported as recorded rather than treated as a separate presidential term.[8]

The NARA board also contains a separate empty card titled “ArnieVinick, 42nd.” That ordinal conflicts with the compiled presidential table, which lists Cyxlinder as the 42nd President. The available records do not reconcile the two entries, so the separate card is not treated here as evidence of another Arnie presidency.[9]
