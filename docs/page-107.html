NARA Presidential Records roster

Jtport was the forty-sixth President of the United States, succeeding Bakedgoods and preceding TacticalRock101. His presidency included a 2022 impeachment trial ending in acquittal. He also served in the legal profession and judiciary and became National Elections Administrator in June 2025.[2]

Legal service

A Supreme Court Bar Membership card states that Jtport was admitted to the bar on February 2, 2021, following two months in the inferior bar and on the recommendation of Justice Barrett.[3]

Presidency

Jtport's presidential service fell between Bakedgoods's second administration and TacticalRock101's return to office. The Senate acquitted him on all charges on 22 October 2022, and TacticalRock101 was publicly welcomed as President by 30 October. The exact transition date remains unspecified.[4]

Later service

On May 20, 2025, Group Management named Jtport to a three-judge District Court panel convened to decide a dispute over the status of President Rathuzen and the attempted impeachment proceedings. The announcement said the panel would issue a public, binding decision within 72 hours after the case began; it identified Waffles, Jtport, and Moneymack as the judges.[5]

On June 30, 2025, the verified nUSA announcements account said Jtport had been selected as National Elections Administrator. The announcement described him as a jurist of six years' standing and a former President, and said he had the organization's confidence to maintain election integrity.[6]

Impeachment trial and acquittal

Jtport's presidency faced an impeachment trial in 2022. On 22 October, aConArtist announced that the Senate had acquitted him on every charge presented to the chamber. The acquittal distinguishes his case from proceedings that ended in removal, including the earlier Procursive impeachment.[7][8]

The trial took place amid disputes over Senate procedure and administration. Secretary of the Senate hankwim55 resigned that office in September, alleging improper decisions over committee business, proxies and staffing. He remained a senator for Jtport's trial and announced his resignation from the chamber on 2 November after the proceeding had concluded. His criticism reflected a participant's position, rather than an adjudicated finding against Senate leadership.[9]

By 30 October, Speaker MasonRockefeller was welcoming TacticalRock101 and GameLab as President and Vice President. The sequence establishes that Jtport's acquittal preceded the next administration; acquittal itself was not his removal from office. The preserved trial announcement does not supply the articles or usable individual vote totals, which remain unspecified here.[10][11]
