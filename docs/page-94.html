Smallchomama1234 (also identified as SmallCho) was an officeholder in the nUSA roleplay government. The NARA Presidential Records board lists Smallchomama1234 as the 26th President. A contemporaneous announcement records an interim swearing-in on 21 July 2019, following President Bakedgoods's resignation and pending the expected inauguration of President-elect Josh.[2]

Senate and vice presidency

An archived post dated 14 January 2019 reproduced a statement attributed to the Special Counsel's Office under an embedded Twitter heading that identified the account as “Senator SmallCho.” The excerpt preserved in the channel is incomplete, so it documents the title and publication of the statement, not the investigation's findings or disposition.[3] By June, SmallCho was identified as President of the Senate. A Federal Election Commission letter dated 14 June addressed SmallCho in that capacity; the election administrator said on 18 June that the results had been delivered to the Senate President and House Speaker. A later FEC results letter dated 21 October again addressed SmallCho as Senate President.[4]

The July archive also identifies SmallCho as Vice President before the interim oath. On 1 July, an announcement said President wack man had transferred presidential powers to Acting President SmallCho during an absence, initially through 8 July. On 12 July, SmallCho reported that wack man had returned from vacation and resumed presidential duties. These notices establish a temporary acting role distinct from the later July 21 swearing-in. The channel does not explain how that July 12 return relates to the July 21 notice that Bakedgoods was the outgoing President.[5]

Interim presidency

At 6:26 a.m. on 21 July, the channel reported that Bakedgoods had “officially resigned the Presidency” and that Vice President SmallCho would serve until President-elect Josh was sworn in later that day. A post at 6:51 a.m. said SmallCho had been sworn in as President. The channel subsequently announced an inauguration livestream at 11 a.m. and thanked viewers, but the reviewed messages do not preserve the oath itself or a precise time at which SmallCho's interim tenure ended.[6]

By 22 July, Josh was publicly issuing Executive Order J-001. This places the transfer to Josh after SmallCho's brief service, although the cited archive does not record Josh's oath or specify the hour of succession. A NARA monthly update in August 2024 later listed Smallchomama1234's presidency as zero days and ranked it the shortest in its summary; the July announcement itself gives no duration.[7]

The reviewed July notices document SmallCho's oath and succession but do not attribute an executive order, policy announcement, or other presidential action to him during the interim period. Later that summer, the archive named him as NARA Archivist in an update to the Public Law Database. On 7 September, the Senate President Pro Tempore announced that SmallCho had passed confirmation for Vice President by a 12–0 vote; SmallCho's response said he would “once again” serve in that office. The NARA Presidential Records board also has later Vice President roster cards for Smallchomama1234, though the cited cards give no term dates.[8]
