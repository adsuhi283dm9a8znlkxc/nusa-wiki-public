Technozo is an American politician who served as the 31st President of nUSA from 27 June to 21 July 2020.[1] He was elected Vice President alongside Reset4K in the Winter 2020 election and succeeded him as President in June. He was succeeded by BernardCaldwell. His career has also included service as mayor of Washington, D.C., a member of Congress, Secretary of Transportation, Speaker of the House, and an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court.[2]

Presidential succession

Technozo ran with reset3634643 (also known as Reset4K) in the Winter 2020 election. The Federal Election Commission certified Reset4K as President-elect and Technozo as Vice President-elect on 17 February 2020.[3]

Technozo was sworn in as the 38th Vice President on 21 March 2020, alongside Reset4K as the 30th President. He was also scheduled to give an inaugural address at the ceremony.[4]

Technozo succeeded Reset4K as President in June 2020 and served until July. BernardCaldwell won the subsequent presidential election, which was certified on 5 July, and was inaugurated as the 32nd President on 21 July.[5]

Earlier roles

Technozo was appointed mayor of Washington, D.C. in February 2018 after the planned mayoral elections failed. He also entered the presidential race that May but withdrew after HHPrinceGeorge re-entered the contest.[6]

Technozo served as a clan manager in 2018 and later held office as a representative, senator, and Senate majority leader. He also served as Secretary of Transportation and was Speaker of the House in July 2024.[7]

Judicial service, retirement, and congressional recognition

Under the name Justice Fortas, Technozo was appointed an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court by President Teasoups and sworn in on 17 August 2024. He announced his retirement on 7 October, effective 9 October 2024.[8]

Congress enacted the Technozo Congressional Gold Medal Act as Public Law 100-3 in recognition of his presidential, congressional, executive, and judicial service. The act authorized a gold medal for Technozo and directed the Speaker and Senate President pro tempore to arrange its presentation.[9]

Technozo was nominated again for Associate Justice in a White House letter dated 10 October 2025. The Senate approved the nomination. The date of his subsequent swearing-in and the duration of his second judicial term are uncertain.[10]
