Aerium was the seventh President of the United States, serving from 23 September to 29 November 2014. He later served as a Clan Manager and election administrator in 2019, receiving a trial appointment in July and a full appointment in August with responsibility for election, military, and community portfolios.[2]

Clan Management and elections

A 9 July 2019 public notice designated Aerium a trial Clan Manager for thirty days, with full appointment to follow at the end of the trial. The accompanying letter described the group's appointment process and the trial's connection to a planned handover of election and military oversight duties.[3]

On 13 August, the announcement said Aerium had fully ascended to Clan Manager and would take over the National Elections Administrator and Military Clan Manager posts, as well as a Community Clan Manager assignment. The linked letter described the National Elections Administrator as the senior overseer or commissioner of the Federal Election Commission (nUSA), responsible for elections, rules, and procedures.[4]

The election records provide examples of this portfolio in practice: a May 2019 letter certifies Senate Class III results, and a June letter delivers House Class I results to the outgoing Speaker. Both documents bear Aerium's name and are linked from election-result announcements.[5]

Later government service

A July 2019 Senate bi-weekly report identified Aerium as a former President selected to serve as Senate President Pro Tempore. The report also summarized Senate activity, including consideration of the National Archives Reformation Act, placing Aerium's later Senate role in the period when the group was discussing archival organization.[6]

Election documentation

The Senate results letter reported seven winners in the Class III contest and stated that voters disqualified by the election process would be placed on a voting blacklist. The House Class I letter delivered twelve winners to the outgoing Speaker and likewise described a blacklist for disqualified voters. These are statements within the election documents about their own procedures; the article does not repeat the names or circumstances of individual voters.[7]

The sequence links formal staff appointment with concrete records work: as elections lead, Aerium's letters moved results to the relevant legislative recipients, while the Senate report places Aerium in a separate parliamentary post later that summer. This distinction matters because the Group Manager, National Elections Administrator, Military Clan Manager, Community Clan Manager, and Senate President Pro Tempore were different roleplay positions, each documented in a different type of record.[8]
