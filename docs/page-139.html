2025 presidential elections (nUSA)

  • Page
  • Discussion

From nUSA Wiki

The 2025 nUSA presidential elections included a March election and later July and November contests. The records distinguish schedules, certified results, and public oath announcements; they do not fully explain every transition or roster ordinal.

March election

Main article: Elections in nUSA

The Federal Election Commission's 13 March ballot notice listed Pioneer Party nominees Rathuzen and snowbleed, Forward Party nominees aConArtist and Frosty_TheSn0wman, and independent candidates FighterHath and warlockings. Voting was scheduled for 14–16 March, with inauguration planned for 23 March.[1]

The 16 March results reported 444 ballots, 44 filtered out, and 400 valid. The Rathuzen–snowbleed ticket received 60.75% (269 ballots; 243 valid); aConArtist–Frosty_theSn0wman received 28.0% (123; 112 valid); and FighterHath–warlockings received 11.25% (52; 45 valid). The FEC declared Rathuzen and snowbleed President-elect and Vice President-elect for a term scheduled to begin 23 March and end 20 July.[2]

On 23 March, an owner-affiliated post advertised Rathuzen's inauguration as happening; later announcements publicly identified Rathuzen as President and snowbleed as Vice President. The inspected posts do not contain oath text.[3]

July election

The July presidential schedule changed during the campaign. The initial notice scheduled voting for 11–13 July and described a term beginning 20–26 July, ending 22 November. On 5 July, the National Elections Administrator extended registration, allowed a Forward Party primary, and moved voting to 18–20 July; the 18 July schedule described a term beginning 21 July and ending 21 November.[4]

The 20 July NEA Discord post named @zapz. as President-elect and linked a certificate that instead names aConArtist the 56th President. The accessible one-page certificate lists 99 votes for aConArtist, 72 for Fighterhath, 25 for Saviorjosh, and one each for Rammarchangel and Old_dude47. A separate inaugural notice identifies aConArtist and TacticalRock101 as President-elect and Vice President-elect and schedules their ceremony for 25 July. The owner later announced that aConArtist's inauguration was starting. The inspected July records do not include an oath transcript or explicit completion notice.[5]

November election

On 23 October, a public notice named Bathamza as the Pioneer Party nominee and ArmanFoley as the Forward Party nominee; independent candidates could register beginning 7 November. The NEA's 4 November notice scheduled voting for 14–16 November and said the term would begin 21 November and end 21 March 2026.[6]

Mallsoft appeared on the 14 November ballot and was disqualified that day. The announcement does not state the reason. The results document certified Bathamza as President-elect and the 58th President, with 111 votes; it did not name a Vice President. On 22 November, the NEA announced that Bathamza had just been sworn in as President alongside Vice President aConArtist. The published schedule's 21 November start and the reported 22 November oath date differ by one day.[7]

Roster and transition limits

NARA's roster lists Rathuzen as 54th, snowbleed as 55th, Mallsoft as 56th, and aConArtist as 57th. The accessible July FEC certificate calls aConArtist the 56th President; November results identify Bathamza as 58th. The checked sources do not explain the ordinal differences or establish a dated Mallsoft accession. The roster labels should be reported as NARA entries rather than treated as a complete succession history.[8]
