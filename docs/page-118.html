NARA Presidential Records roster

jsjsjbsd is the fifty-ninth President of the United States. He assumed presidential duties following bathamza's resignation in June 2026 and won the summer presidential election. His administration consolidated military operations and opposed restrictions on executive appointments through legislative vetoes.[2]

Earlier elections and offices

The Federal Election Commission's July 2023 results announcement named jsjsjbsd among six winning Senate candidates. Later in 2023, it included the name among major candidates invited to a November presidential debate. The archive excerpt establishes an invitation, not an election victory in that contest. A separate local ballot placed jsjsjbsd among candidates for a council seat, followed by an ineligibility notice saying the candidate had resigned the candidacy; the two records concern a different election from the presidential debate.[3]

The congressional nominations archive records a passed nomination for jsjsjbsd as Secretary of State. Its card title supports Senate action but does not establish a full tenure. A 2025 concurrent resolution proposed appointment as Auditor General after asserting that the President had not submitted a candidate list within the required window. The attached two-page text sets out a two-month office and investigatory and reporting duties, but it contains a placeholder for the date of presentation and inconsistent resolution labels. The board places the item under Concurrent Resolutions; without an enactment record, the document should be read as the text of a proposed appointment rather than conclusive proof that jsjsjbsd served the term.[4]

The Senate records board also has archived membership cards for jsjsjbsd. A later passed-nominations card names jsjsjbsd for Vice President. The records consulted here do not give an oath date for that vice presidency or explain how it relates to the subsequent 59th-presidency roster entry. Senate and congressional listings show a multi-office career, but the available cards cannot support an unbroken chronology of service.[5]

Presidency and limits of the record

jsjsjbsd succeeded bathamza in June 2026. His administration subsequently issued Executive Order 9 on military consolidation and vetoed S. 1 and S. 2 in July. An invitation scheduled his inauguration ceremony for 22 July, although the exact time of the oath remains unspecified.[6]

The Supreme Court board includes a petition naming jsjsjbsd as respondent in a case brought by Rand0mAxis, and another case filed by jsjsjbsd in an earlier court archive. These docket titles establish litigation involving the account, not the merits or outcomes of the cases. The congressional board also contains a concurrent-resolution card on a congressional gold medal for jsjsjbsd and a failed card proposing removal of one; their titles and list locations do not prove that a medal was awarded or taken away.[7]


June 2026 succession

President bathamza's 8 June 2026 resignation statement named jsjsjbsd as the leader of the next administration. After that day's Senate election result, The War Room reported that Vice President jsjsjbsd had assumed the presidency under the Twenty-fifth Amendment but had not yet been sworn in. N-SPAN described him the following morning as acting President pending an oath. These reports and bathamza's letter establish the announced transition; the reviewed sources do not supply the oath record or exact time at which acting service became a regular presidency.[8]

2026 presidential election

In the Summer 2026 presidential election, jsjsjbsd ran as an independent. The final ballot listed no vice-presidential running mate for the candidacy, although the FEC had earlier announced receipt of a letter naming ZhangJinping. The FEC said it would not add that pick to the ballot and that a Vice President would have to be nominated under law if jsjsjbsd won. One opponent, FuriousTurtle06, withdrew after voting opened; the FEC said voters could not recast ballots.[9]

On 16 July, the National Elections Administrator certified jsjsjbsd as President-elect with 131 votes, ahead of the Pioneer ticket of Epicwarrior_99 and Anthony R. Burgess (114) and the Forward ticket of AguilaBread and EdmundCarter (5). The FEC stated that 297 ballots had been cast and 254 were valid, although the itemized candidate totals sum to 250. The election schedule had set a presidential term from 21 July to 21 November 2026; these are announced term dates, and the reviewed FEC export contains no oath or inauguration report. On 22 July the FEC said a Vice President needed to be nominated but did not name a nominee or record confirmation. A later 14 September notice opened campaigning for the November 2026 election and said that registration and voting dates would follow; the reviewed export includes no result or successor for that race.[10]

The Spring 2026 contest also involved jsjsjbsd as a Forward primary candidate: Warlockings won that primary 22 votes to 18. The FEC later imposed a 45-day blacklist after reporting that two non-citizens had voted in the primary, but on 17 March it announced that a successful appeal reduced the blacklist to one day, already served. The primary investigation and sanction did not disqualify jsjsjbsd from the later Summer presidential race in the records reviewed here.[11]

Military consolidation and legislative vetoes

In July 2026, jsjsjbsd ordered a major military reorganization. An order dated 8 July suspended independent Marine Corps and Air Force operations and recruitment, directing Marine personnel into the Army and Air Force personnel into the Navy. Air Force special-operations personnel were assigned to Naval Special Warfare Command. The President announced the measure on 9 July following discussions with military leadership.[12][13]

The order preserved transferred personnel's ranks, awards and service records. It also kept the suspended branches in legal existence, permitting their restoration by a later executive order or congressional act. The administration thus concentrated active military operations while protecting both individual standing and the possibility of institutional revival.[14]

On 21 July, jsjsjbsd vetoed S. 1 and S. 2. He objected to S. 1's treatment of public-servant neutrality and to S. 2's restrictions on acting appointments and the filling of vacancies. His veto message characterized both as legislative encroachments on executive authority and stressed the ability to maintain a functioning administration. Those constitutional objections were his stated grounds for rejecting the bills, rather than a Supreme Court judgment on them.[15]

That day's inauguration invitation scheduled a ceremony for 22 July at the Capitol's East Portico. The invitation supplies a planned ceremony date, separate from the electoral term schedule and earlier assumption of presidential duties; it does not establish the precise time the oath was actually administered.[16]
