NARA Presidential Records roster

Mallsoft is listed by NARA as the 56th President of the Roblox nUSA roleplay government. The surviving presidential roster places Mallsoft between snowbleed and aConArtist, but its card does not record an oath or term dates. Mallsoft also appears in records of Group Management, the vice presidency, and the community's courts. These records span several years and do not by themselves form an uninterrupted account of one office.[1]

Earlier administrative and judicial roles

Mallsoft appears in early nUSA Discord material as a clan manager. During 2019 and 2020, management announcements directed community-engagement questions to Mallsoft and sought ideas for activities intended to increase participation. These notices document an administrative role and stated outreach plans, but they do not establish the result of the proposals.[2]

The congressional nominations archive has entries for Mallsoft as Vice President, Associate Justice of the Supreme Court, Chief District Court Judge, and Chief Judge. Those cards are filed among Passed Nominations, yet their titles alone do not establish the dates or length of service in each office. A Supreme Court former-member card specifically lists Justice Mallsoft in an associate-justice seat from 5 December 2024 to 24 January 2025 and names President Teasoups as the appointing President. The court record offers a firmer date range for that judicial term than the nomination titles do for the other posts.[3]

Presidency and later records

The NARA card identifies Mallsoft as the 56th President, following snowbleed. The reviewed presidential board does not attach an executive order, memorandum, election certificate, or inauguration announcement to the profile. Consequently the sources available here do not justify exact term dates or attribution of a specific policy to Mallsoft's administration. The successor in the roster is aConArtist, whose inauguration was announced for July 25, 2025; the announcement does not itself say when Mallsoft ceased to hold office.[4]

The congressional archive also contains a measure titled Impeaching Mallsoft, Chief Judge of the District Court. Its wording concerns Mallsoft's judicial office, not the presidency. A title and archived card do not establish that its allegations were proven or that the measure removed Mallsoft. Separate court records index disciplinary matters and litigation involving the account; their existence is not a substitute for reading the outcomes of those cases.[5]


2025 FEC election records

NARA's Presidential Records roster lists Mallsoft as the 56th President. In the checked 2025 election records, Mallsoft appears on the 14 November Winter Presidential Election ballot and was disqualified later that day. The notice gives no reason. No dated election result, succession notice, or oath establishing Mallsoft's presidential service was located in the reviewed source set. NARA's ordinal is an archival listing, not enough by itself to establish a term or transition; it also conflicts with the July 2025 FEC certificate calling aConArtist the 56th President.[6]
