BernardCaldwell is an American politician who served as the 20th and 32nd President of nUSA. Before becoming President, he was elected to the House of Representatives in May 2017.[2] His first Presidential term lasted from 21 to 26 July 2018, and his second from 21 July to 16 September 2020.[1] His first term was marked by a war with the United Kingdom and impeachment by the House of Representatives. He returned to office after winning the Summer 2020 election with Lacryma. His second administration pursued changes to law enforcement and executive-branch oversight and issued an ultimatum to the United Kingdom. He also faced several sanctions imposed by Clan Management.[3]


His involvement in the rival United States groups HUSA and BUSA became a recurring source of controversy in nUSA. Clan Management regarded BUSA as a continuation of HUSA and accused its organizers of threatening the community's existence. In October 2020, it permanently banned BernardCaldwell and other BUSA principals and disqualified them from nUSA citizenship.[4]

2017 House campaign and election

On 2 May 2017, BernardCaldwell published a campaign speech seeking election to the House of Representatives. He criticized legislators' attendance and pledged to remain active and attend most congressional sessions.[5]

His platform proposed a constitutional amendment strengthening voter requirements, electoral reform, stronger protections for Second Amendment rights, and the repeal of regulations he considered unnecessary. He also proposed legislation requiring congressional attendance and national security reforms that would expand law enforcement jurisdiction in cities.[5]

On 8 May 2017, the Federal Elections Commission certified BernardCaldwell's election to House seat 23 of 24 with 20 votes. The certificate recognized him as a United States Representative and provided for a two-month term scheduled to expire on 8 July 2017. It was signed by David E. Racine, III, as Commission Overseer and Archivist of the United States.[2]

First presidency and 2018 impeachment

BernardCaldwell, also known as MrCaldwellRBX, was sworn in as President on 21 July 2018 in a ceremony held on Discord. Two days earlier, a congressional resolution had proposed censuring him over allegations that he promised appointments to campaign supporters. The resolution was referred to committee; its subsequent outcome is uncertain.[6]

War with the United Kingdom

BernardCaldwell's first presidency coincided with a war between nUSA and the United Kingdom in July 2018. On 22 July, an emergency joint session of Congress considered a declaration of war following a briefing about an alleged infiltration. Article II of the subsequent impeachment resolution accused BernardCaldwell of giving Congress faulty evidence and later blaming legislators for the escalation. It stated that the Senate initially approved a war resolution, but the House rejected it and the Senate then rejected it on reconsideration. The resolution also accused him of privately urging war while negotiating with the British government.[7]

On 24 July, BernardCaldwell published a statement addressing a British ultimatum and asked citizens whether nUSA should go to war with the United Kingdom. The following day, PatrickCoyne introduced H.J. Res. 2, proposing a formal declaration of war. Its preamble stated that the British government had already declared war on the United States, and its operative provisions would authorize the President to use the country's military forces and resources against Britain. The resolution was referred to the House Armed Services and Veteran Affairs Committee; its final legislative outcome is uncertain.[8]

Fighting was reported on 25 July, when a public announcement stated that nUSA had lost its first raid by a score of 101–35. That evening, Lordsights condemned the war as having begun under false pretenses and said he could no longer support either the conflict or BernardCaldwell. The handling of the British dispute became a central issue in the House's impeachment proceedings the next day.[9][7]

Impeachment and eligibility disputes

On 26 July 2018, the House Judiciary Committee advanced an impeachment resolution against BernardCaldwell. The House passed the resolution, H. Res. 12, and referred it to the Senate. Its three articles accused him of unlawfully promising appointments, misconduct in a diplomatic dispute with the United Kingdom and France, and misconduct relating to a prospective judicial nominee's membership in another United States group. The outcome of the Senate proceedings is uncertain.[10]

In October 2018, the Senate lifted BernardCaldwell's disqualification from holding office. Later that month, the Federal Election Commission separately disqualified him as a presidential candidate. The grounds for the FEC decision were not specified in its announcement.[11]

Return to office in 2020

BernardCaldwell won the Summer 2020 presidential election as the Constitutionalist Party candidate, with Lacryma as his running mate. He received 280 votes; the National Elections Administrator reported 569 ballots received and 101 manually disqualified. The result was certified on 5 July, and BernardCaldwell and Lacryma were inaugurated on 21 July.[12]

During his second term, BernardCaldwell issued executive orders on law enforcement and administrative reform. An order dated 14 August called for a joint task force to protect the District of Columbia spawn area and World War II memorial and coordinate domestic-security agencies. Another established an Internal Affairs Institute to investigate executive-branch employees, excluding violations of Clan Management's community guidelines from its jurisdiction. Subsequent orders directed agencies to create Discord channels for crime reports, established probationary training standards for federal law-enforcement recruits, and called for work on a United States Employment Center.[13]

August 2020 United Kingdom ultimatum

On 6 August 2020, BernardCaldwell signed a joint nUSA–Zimbabwe ultimatum to the United Kingdom. It demanded apologies, the playing of the United States anthem in Parliament, cooperation in the United Nations, and the reopening of diplomatic channels. The ultimatum threatened a state of war if the demands were not accepted by 7 August. A declaration of war was proposed in Congress on 7 August, but its passage and any subsequent fighting are uncertain. The dispute occurred during BernardCaldwell's second term, after the April 2020 war under Reset4K.[14]

Management sanctions and splinter-group disputes

HUSA and the 2019 blacklist

BernardCaldwell's involvement with HUSA drew criticism from nUSA members in 2019. On 19 April, Real_CoolAid described him as HUSA's President. On 24 April, PatrioticCross accused him and HHPrinceGeorge of leaving nUSA to develop HUSA and recruiting developers for the rival group. Contemporary discussion also described HUSA as competing with nUSA for members and activity.[15]

On 10 May 2019, Clan Management banned and blacklisted BernardCaldwell, HHPrinceGeorge, Joyiscold, Helleoh, and ApolloKnowles for six months, allowing appeals after three months. Members linked the sanctions to HUSA, and management's later BUSA announcement referred to an earlier “HUSA blacklist.” The May announcement itself did not specify the underlying conduct.[16]

Suspension during his second presidency

During BernardCaldwell's second presidency, management imposed a one-week blacklist in August 2020, alleging harassment and assistance to a blacklisted person's alternate account. Management stated that the Vice President would exercise presidential powers during the suspension.[17]

BUSA and permanent removal

On 21 October 2020, Clan Management announced sanctions against the organizers of BUSA, which it described as a new version of the HUSA remake. A letter addressed to BernardCaldwell and signed by Clan Manager David E. Racine, III, identified him among the movement's principals and stated that he and others had worked to create a new United States group. Management characterized their actions as “rebellion and insurrection” and as harmful to nUSA's continued existence.[4]

The order imposed permanent bans and citizenship disqualification, without appeal, on BernardCaldwell, Asceylos, RichoCaldwell, cashcursive, RobCaldwell, Technozo, and other BUSA Presidents, developers, and group-management members. The letter distinguished management's disciplinary authority from government proceedings concerning rebellion and stated that those proceedings would not affect its decision. The accompanying announcement exempted ordinary BUSA citizens from a blacklist unless they advertised the group or attempted to recruit nUSA members. It separately cited constitutional restrictions on officeholding for participants who had previously taken an oath of office.[4]

BUSA's activity soon attracted criticism. On 28 October, Pauljkl said its games had “died on day 1,” blaming their developers. In November, Bancachuli also described the group as inactive. These comments reflected members' assessments of BUSA's decline; its formal closure date is uncertain.[18]

The conflict continued beyond BUSA. On 19 December 2021, largeTitanic2 announced that membership restrictions comparable to those imposed on BUSA would apply to another United States group created by BernardCaldwell. Members were given until the following Tuesday to leave. The notice alleged earlier attacks on Trello boards, Discord servers, and groups; it did not name the new organization.[19]

House speakership

BernardCaldwell was announced as the newly elected Speaker on 2 January 2020, with RichoCaldwell elected House Majority Leader.[20] By 12 January, Caldwell was serving as Vice President and the White House was addressing TacusLord as Speaker. The precise date of the transition between these offices is uncertain.[21]
