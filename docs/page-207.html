The November 2024 Presidential Election in nUSA returned Teasoups to the presidency. The Pioneer ticket of Teasoups and Rathuzen received 184 votes, ahead of ApolloKorman and MitsuoMochizuki with 82. The FEC and Group Management certified the result on 10 November.[1][2][3]

Campaign

The campaigning period opened on 10 October. FTN News reported that incumbent President Teasoups and Nasanovuh had launched campaigns; the same outlet reported a merger of the Plutocratic and Forward parties, with Forward continuing and former Plutocratic caucus members becoming independents.[4] An 18 October campaign announcement PDF said Nasanovuh intended to run on a Plutocratic ticket, while an Inside Edition News post on 14 October had said that Nasanovuh's campaign was suspended and that he endorsed ApolloKorman. The campaign announcement and suspension report conflict; the timing of Nasanovuh’s withdrawal is uncertain.[5]

By 1 November, news posts identified three major tickets: Teasoups with Rathuzen, ApolloKorman with MitsuoMochizuki, and Forward nominee TradeNato with Razvanpaul. The FEC’s final notice lists TradeNato among candidates who withdrew on 8 November; the results page lists the remaining candidates and their vote totals.[6]

The ApolloKorman campaign received a warning from the FEC on 15 October after a complaint alleged that a campaign leader used Discord announcement channels in other communities to urge members to join nUSA and vote for ApolloKorman and another candidate. Reuters News described the FEC as finding repeated campaign announcements; the linked FEC/Group Management warning itself is the primary source for the commission’s stated action.[7]

During the campaign, Teasoups’s campaign released a defense and military platform. The document proposed changes to nUSA institutions and agencies, including the Department of Defense and intelligence organizations; the proposals did not establish that those changes were enacted.[8]

Election and candidates

The 7 November ballot also listed TradeNato, stratnixon, Sheev_Palpatine with AdremiCross, and Joobz2121 with EmperorXD. It described ApolloKorman as independent, while the result certificate labeled his ticket Forward. The certificate did not explain the changed affiliation or stratnixon’s absence from its final distribution. adamsoups appeared in the certificate but not in that ballot notice.[1][2][3]

Voting

The election opened on 8 November, with Reuters News reporting that voting would close on 10 November at 3 p.m. Eastern Time. On election day, outlets posted competing calls before the FEC announcement: Fact Feed Network prematurely called the race for ApolloKorman at 1:17 p.m., while The American Times projected a Teasoups win thirteen minutes later. These were media reports and projections, not the official result.[9]

The four continuing-candidate entries total 275 votes. The certificate separately recorded 27 votes for withdrawn TradeNato and six for withdrawn Sheev_PalpatineTGR/AdremiCross, bringing the itemized total to the stated 308 post-filter voters. It reported 18 new voters; pre-filter turnout and invalidations were not stated.[1][2][3]

Certification and transition

The certificate scheduled the winning ticket’s inauguration for 23 November. Contemporary news reported that Teasoups and Rathuzen took their oaths that day; the electoral certification and inauguration were distinct events.[1][2][3]

Inauguration

The FEC results notice scheduled the inauguration for 23 November 2024, nearly two weeks after the result announcement. The American Times reported that Teasoups and Rathuzen took their oaths that day, describing Teasoups as beginning a second term and the 53rd President in the group’s sequence. The result announcement and the later reported swearing-in were separate events.[10]
