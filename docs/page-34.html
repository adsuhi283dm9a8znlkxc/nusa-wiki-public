2025 constitutional readoption in nUSA

  • Page
  • Discussion
Appearance
From nUSA Wiki

A NARA public post dated 16 May 2025 reported that a Constitution associated with Timothy F. Geithner had been readopted and revised through Public Law 105-1 (S.J. Res. 14) on 12 May. The NARA Hub's “TimCons 09-22-18” card links a document whose cover identifies it as authored on 22 September 2018 and re-adopted on 12 May 2025. A separate Public Law Database entry gives the constitutional text an 8 September 2017 origin and an additional 17 January 2024 readoption date; the available sources do not reconcile these versions.[1]

Announcement and document

The NARA public announcement attributes the Constitution's development to Timothy F. Geithner in late 2018 and says the text was readopted with revisions by P.L. 105-1, identified as S.J. Res. 14. Zhang Jinping signed the announcement as Archivist. The dated post documents NARA's account of the process; it does not independently establish details absent from the underlying resolution or a complete convention history.[2]

The attached 17-page PDF cover says “TimCons 09-22-18,” gives 22 September 2018 as the authorship date and 12 May 2025 as the readoption date. The NARA Hub marks that card “Active Constitution.” These are document and board status statements; they support identifying the archived copy but do not erase the discrepant 2017 and January 2024 dates found in the separate PLD entry.[3]

Version and status questions

The Public Law Database history card describes a constitution adopted on 8 September 2017, readopted on 17 January 2024 and readopted again on 12 May 2025. The Hub, in contrast, names a September 2018 TimCons version. The records may concern a related text lineage or different drafts, but no accessible source here explains the discrepancy. Accordingly, this article reports both attributions rather than treating either origin date as settled.[4]

The July 2023 Constitution is a distinct archived version, published and instituted on 17 July 2023 and labeled inactive on the Hub snapshot. The 2024 “GobiesCons” record is also labeled inactive, while TimCons bears the active label. This pattern suggests the Hub's recorded status at export time, but board labels do not supply a complete legal transition history or explain the exact effect of P.L. 105-1.[5]

Relationship to NARA and prior versions

The 2023 Constitution expressly assigns NARA records responsibilities. The 19-page May 2025 Constitution PDF was inspected, but it contains no searchable NARA clause. That absence alone cannot establish that NARA's authority was removed or changed; the documents may address the agency differently, or the PDF text may omit relevant language in a way not captured by extraction.[6]

The 2025 readoption is part of a broader sequence that includes the July 2023 legal reset and a Constitution announced as passed in November 2024. The sources reviewed do not state in one place how the 2024 text transitioned to the 2025 version. See Constitutions of nUSA, 2024 Constitution of nUSA and 2023 nUSA legal reset.[7]
