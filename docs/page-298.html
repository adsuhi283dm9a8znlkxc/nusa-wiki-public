Squareheaddude, also known as Square, is a developer, designer and administrator whose work helped shape Washington, D.C., Las Vegas and Ridgeway. A longtime member of United States Clan Management, he combined development with moderation, municipal administration and public communication. He co-founded Ridgeway with Pauljkl and DevGralius and led its map design, visual presentation and equipment development.[1][2][3]

As of October 2026, he remains a member of Ridgeway's Developer Oversight and Superintendent of the Ridgeway State Police. His public profile identifies him as Ridgeway's creator.[4]

Development in the United States

Las Vegas and the rebuilding of Washington

Squareheaddude worked on the development and upkeep of the nation's cities alongside Pauljkl. In 2017, he solicited opinions about Las Vegas vehicles, maintained additions to its music application and publicized Nevada Highway Patrol recruitment. These activities connected the city's transport and public services with direct feedback from citizens.[5]

His major Washington project was the rebuilt capital released on 1 December 2017. He publicized its trailer in November, announced the opening and began collecting bug reports immediately after release. The new city subsequently became known as Washington's second generation, or DC2.[1][6]

Development continued after the opening. In December, Squareheaddude recruited leadership for the Metropolitan Police Department and announced the release of a fire update and revised vehicle controls. In November 2018, he advertised a large update and a proposed trucking-delivery system. The trucking announcement described a planned civilian occupation rather than establishing when it was implemented.[7]

Public testing and municipal institutions

Squareheaddude's work also encompassed the institutions that operated within the capital. In February 2018, he announced Technozo's appointment as mayor after the planned mayoral election fell through and organized publicity for the new city's council elections. In 2019, he published municipal election schedules and announced council results.[8]

He used public testing and surveys to inform further development. A June 2019 survey asked citizens about hypothetical additions, removals and preferences, explicitly distinguishing those questions from promised updates. A September test focused on reducing vehicle instability, improving the introduction for new citizens and supporting larger servers. These initiatives made public participation part of the development process.[9]

Clan Management and community administration

Squareheaddude served as a Clan Manager by October 2017. His responsibilities included moderation recruitment, enforcement announcements and the organization of the national communications server. In August 2017, he announced maintenance and automated role verification; later notices concerned moderator applications, account security and the promotion of probationary moderators.[3][10]

His administrative position placed him between technical management and elected government. A September 2020 letter proposed a reconciliation conference between Clan Management and the executive, legislative and judicial branches, naming Squareheaddude and Pauljkl among those involved in arranging it. In November, he created a dedicated agency-application channel at the administration's request, while leaving the selection of approved departments to executive leadership.[11][12]

Ridgeway

Founding and design

Ridgeway began as a joint development project by Squareheaddude, Pauljkl and DevGralius. Squareheaddude's responsibilities included the map and terrain, vehicles, graphics, logos, interfaces, weapons and tools. Pauljkl and DevGralius concentrated on backend systems, scripted features and optimization. Although their later accounts disagreed about the partnership's breakdown, both described this division between building and scripting.[2][13]

In January 2020, he outlined a new project intended to offer a more realistic approach to policing while retaining Washington's and Las Vegas's existing styles. Ridgeway's charter was adopted on 4 July. He publicized a preview stream in September and announced the county's public launch on 21 November.[14][15][16][17]

His launch work included instructions for overcoming a purchase failure and announcements about bug fixes, the National Guard and the Law Enforcement Training Center. He subsequently announced access-price reductions to 500 Robux in December 2020 and 100 Robux in January 2021, followed by a free-access weekend in April.[18]

Separation and reunion of Developer Oversight

The original development partnership broke down in January 2022, amid disagreements about administration, moderation and the project's direction. Squareheaddude issued a memorandum on 14 January describing his account of the conflict; Pauljkl published a response. The separation ended the three-person partnership and accompanied Ridgeway's departure from the United States. Their competing explanations did not establish every allegation as fact.[2][13]

On 17 January 2024, Squareheaddude announced that the developers had reconciled and that Pauljkl had relinquished ownership of nUSA to return to Ridgeway full-time. The renewed division of responsibilities placed Pauljkl in charge of scripting and backend development, Squareheaddude in charge of game design and graphics, and David, or DevGralius, in charge of web services and government.[19]

The announcement proposed incorporating suitable quality-of-life features and backend improvements from Boulder County into Ridgeway. It described intended work and a request to reopen the county, rather than confirming the completion date of every proposed change.[19]

Later service

Squareheaddude also served in Ridgeway's police leadership. A 2023 biographical entry dates his appointment as Superintendent of the Ridgeway State Police to 5 May 2021. The public departmental roster continued to list him as Superintendent in October 2026, alongside his Developer Oversight membership in the main Ridgeway group and several of its institutions.[20][4]
