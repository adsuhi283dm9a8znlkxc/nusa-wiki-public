From nUSA Wiki

pauljkl is a founder and former owner of the nUSA (Roblox group) whose documented contributions include developing the community's Las Vegas game and overseeing a transfer of group ownership in 2024. Archived group communications identify him as nUSA's founder and describe his work on the Las Vegas city experience.[1]

Founder and game development

An October 2017 announcement described Las Vegas as nUSA's primary city and credited pauljkl with updating, maintaining, and adding features to it. The same announcement's author was a Clan Manager, making the statement contemporary praise by a group official rather than a technical changelog or independent measure of the game's popularity.[2]

A 2019 group announcement reported that, after discussion, pauljkl appointed himself to the roleplay Federal Reserve Board of Governors and assigned Federal Reserve Police members a law-enforcement role limited to protecting the in-game Federal Reserve Bank. The announcement described this as being pursuant to Public Law 47-3; it records the group's stated roleplay arrangement, not a real-world appointment.[3]

Ownership handover

On 17 January 2024, pauljkl announced that he had agreed to transfer ownership of nUSA to UKR41NIAN and Dilliuss, whom he described as successor co-owners. The statement also said Boulder County and its associated games would be discontinued after the transfer, and framed the change as the end of his tenure as founder.[4]

A roster reproduced in the announcements archive lists pauljkl as “Founder” in Group Management with a hire date of 24 April 2022. Because that roster records an administrative entry rather than the group's creation, it is best read as a dated staff-record field, not as the founding date of nUSA.[5]

The founder's role also touched records administration. A 2020 retirement letter from Clan Manager DevGralius said the archives had been under Clan Management, then relinquished them from that structure to Archivist LargeTitanic2. It said a future Archivist could ask pauljkl or Squareheaddude whether the Archives should return to Clan Management. This describes an owner's role in the governance framework for records; it does not identify pauljkl as the Archivist or as the day-to-day operator of the Archives.[6]

A later Group Management tribute gives one view of the relationship between ownership and NARA staffing. In August 2024, the retirement tribute for TacticalRummy stated that Rummy began as NARA Administrator under pauljkl on 28 February 2022, before being promoted to the Group Manager team. Because this date is retrospective, it documents how the 2024 announcement described the history rather than a contemporaneous 2022 appointment notice.[7]

The records therefore show different aspects of the founder's legacy: an early role in the game's development, authority over a later ownership handover, and a connection to the management structure surrounding archival administration. They do not establish that he continued to hold an operational office after the January 2024 transfer announcement.[8]

Stewardship and succession

The January 2024 post characterized the transfer as the end of pauljkl's tenure as founder and asked the community to welcome the new owners. It also framed the group as the result of a long-running community effort rather than a single game project, while specifically identifying the Boulder County project as one that would end in the transition.[9]
