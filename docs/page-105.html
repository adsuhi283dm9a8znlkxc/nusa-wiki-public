TimidSoul
41st President of nUSA (NARA roster)
In office
29 August 2021 – 3 October 2021[1]
Preceded by LeoVinick
Succeeded by Cyxlinder


NARA Presidential Records roster

TimidSoul is listed by the nUSA Presidential Records board as the community's 41st President. The available contemporary election record identifies him as LeoVinick's vice-presidential running mate in the 2021 Summer election and says the National Elections Administrator declared him vice-President-elect. The roster card does not give a term date, and the primary documents reviewed here do not establish when or how he entered the presidency.[2]

Vice-presidential candidacy

The FEC certified LeoVinick, the Republican candidate, as President-elect after the Summer 2021 election. Its July 12 results letter states that the 12th Amendment's vice-presidential submission requirement had been met on June 26 and identifies TimidSoul as the LeoVinick campaign's vice-presidential candidate. The certification declares him vice-President-elect; it does not document his subsequent oath or describe the duration of his service.[3]

Presidential listing

The NARA Presidential Records board gives TimidSoul the 41st ordinal, between LeoVinick (40th) and Cyxlinder (42nd). The roster card is otherwise blank. The reviewed first-party material does not provide a presidential start or end date, an inauguration notice, an account of presidential decisions, or a documented transition to the next listed officeholder. The ordinal is therefore reported as the archive's classification, while details of this presidency remain undetermined from the sources available here.[4]
