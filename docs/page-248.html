The 2016 nUSA–United Kingdom war was a conflict between nUSA and the United Kingdom during the presidency of SuddenRush12G. Fighting took place in the summer of 2016 and ended with a peace agreement dated 3 August. The agreement acknowledged a British victory with a score of 5–0, provided for a mutual cessation of hostilities, and required American recognition of Hawaii's independence. SuddenRush12G and Secretary of State Lumidas were listed as the American signatories, alongside British Prime Minister SidousThePwnda.[1]

The war also produced a diplomatic crisis involving the United Nations and prompted SuddenRush12G to propose changes to military organization and training. The peace arrangement later became the subject of a congressional dispute, followed by renewed hostilities under President TheySinned in January 2017.[2][3][4]

Declaration and diplomatic crisis

On 21 July 2016, Vice President TimGeithner published a joint resolution titled Declaration of War on the United Kingdom. It proposed authorizing a full military campaign and immediately revoking the alliance between the two countries upon passage. Apteria offered to sponsor and present the resolution in a reply to the proposal. The date of its formal legislative approval is uncertain.[5]

The conflict coincided with a deterioration in relations with the United Nations. In a November account, Geithner, who had served as Vice President during the war and participated in backchannel negotiations, described nUSA's suspension from the organization and the loss of its permanent Security Council status. He attributed the diplomatic rupture to the conduct of an American ambassador, whom he said was subsequently dismissed.[2]

Geithner also described an attempt by EUSA to obtain international recognition in nUSA's place during the war. He stated that the United Kingdom and other UN members continued to defend nUSA's recognition despite the conflict. His later argument for remaining in the UN presented that support as a reason to preserve membership.[2]

Fighting and outcome

American and British forces engaged in combat. In his October report to the National Security Council, SuddenRush12G referred to the recent UK conflict as evidence that large military memberships had not produced cohesive or effective fighting units. He criticized the ability of American forces to operate together against the enemy.[3]

The 3 August peace agreement recorded a 5–0 result in favor of the United Kingdom and stated that the United States would submit to the United Kingdom and King James I and lower its arms. The dates and locations of the individual engagements are uncertain.[1]

Peace agreement

The agreement required the United States Military and British Armed Forces to cease hostilities against one another. It also called for an end to attacks that infringed international law and principles. In exchange for the settlement, Britain was to work toward normalizing relations, support the United States in international organizations including the United Nations, and seek the removal of sanctions against the United States.[1]

The American commitments included:

  • Recognizing Hawaii's independence.
  • A formal apology by Vice President TimGeithner to Tentacools for his actions and remarks.
  • Maintaining open and responsive communication channels to prevent another conflict.
  • Working to normalize relations and strengthen diplomatic cooperation.[1]

The document listed Lumidas and SuddenRush12G as the United States delegation and SidousThePwnda as the United Kingdom delegation. These provisions established the terms of the settlement; the extent to which every commitment was implemented is uncertain.[1]

Aftermath

Military reform proposals

On 23 October 2016, SuddenRush12G used the UK conflict as the basis for proposed military reforms. He advocated smaller, cohesive combat units and criticized training that emphasized formations and obstacle courses over fighting. His recommendations included membership caps of 150 for the Army, 75 for the Marine Corps, and 300 each for the Navy and Air Force, together with a common two-day basic-training program. The report set out recommendations rather than announcing their implementation.[3]

Renewed conflict in 2017

In an address dated 28 January 2017, President TheySinned credited SuddenRush12G, TimGeithner, and Lumidas with negotiating the earlier peace plan. He stated that Congress had overridden SuddenRush12G's veto of the Sovereignty Act on 22 January, nullifying the arrangement. TheySinned reported that Britain had subsequently declared war and begun assembling troops and a coalition. He announced military preparations and negotiations over war terms. This renewed conflict occurred under TheySinned's presidency.[4]
