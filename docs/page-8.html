Presidential Records (nUSA)

  • Page
  • Discussion
Appearance
From nUSA Wiki

The Presidential Records board is a Trello collection linked from the NARA Hub for records associated with the President and Vice President in the nUSA roleplay community. The hub calls its link “Presidential Archives,” while the accessible board is titled “Presidential Records.” It is an organized roleplay collection, not an archive of the real U.S. presidency. [1]

Board organization

The board separates materials into lists for executive orders, memoranda, national-security presidential memoranda, presidential decision and review directives, proclamations, signing statements and veto messages, and pardons. A separate “Translated” list and a “Research” list distinguish those holdings from the main instrument categories. List placement records the board’s cataloguing scheme; it does not by itself show that a document is an original, an operative instrument, or a complete record. [2]

The board also has lists for presidents and vice presidents and for Presidential Libraries. A card titled “NARA Presidential Libraries” describes a library function and assigns archival roles in its own board snapshot. Personnel and descriptions on such cards can change; they are not a basis for an undated account of current officeholders. [3]

Library and community records

In December 2025, a NARA server announcement invited participants to share historical items and stories, submit complaints, record oaths, and allow former presidents and the sitting President to submit documents to presidential libraries. This describes public-facing functions announced for the community server, not proof that all former administrations’ records were collected. [4]

Board scope and archival caveats

The board export reviewed for the archive research snapshot contained 14 lists and 376 cards. The lists separate executive materials by document type and add distinct “Translated” and “Research” areas. Those categories preserve context about how an item was stored or presented, but they do not establish whether a translation is authoritative, whether a research copy is an original, or whether the board contains every record from an administration. [5]

NARA’s August 2024 monthly update offers a dated example of the records breadth the project reported: it listed one public law, four executive orders, and six Supreme Court opinions for July, alongside presidential resignation and succession events. This connects the presidential collection to a wider government recordkeeping program, but the monthly post does not inventory what was stored in each Trello list. [6]

The December 2025 server announcement describes a later public submission route for Presidential Library materials. It asks former presidents and the sitting President to open an inquiry ticket to submit historical documents, with access roles granted through that process. This source documents the announced intake procedure rather than completed library holdings. [7]

The board’s collections sit alongside the broader records network linked by the NARA Hub. Executive materials may relate to legislative and court records indexed elsewhere, but the boards remain distinct collections. See Public Law Database (nUSA) and NUSA archival policy. [8]
