From nUSA Wiki

TacticalRummy is a public official whose service has included the chief justiceship, the National Archives, Group Management, election administration and public outreach. Their NARA service began on 28 February 2022.[1]

NARA and Group Management

TacticalRummy began as a NARA Administrator under pauljkl on 28 February 2022. Their later responsibilities included work through a change of ownership and a transition of archival coordination to AIex, followed by service as a District Court judge.[2]

In July 2023, Group Management issued Memorandum #001, which named NARA and archival requirements for the legislature as separate directives. The same announcement credited TacticalRummy with creating the replacement constitution released after the “Great Reset.”[3] These records show involvement in the community’s archival and constitutional administration, while distinguishing NARA’s institutional work from Group Management’s authority.

A December 2023 notice says TacticalRummy moved from probationary to full-time Group Manager with PaulJKL’s assent. In March 2024, an appointment announcement signed by TacticalRummy elevated AustinBoBoston1 from Judicial Branch Assistant Archivist to Archivist of the United States, documenting TacticalRummy’s part in announcing a later NARA leadership appointment.[4]

Resignation and handoff

On 11 August 2024, TacticalRummy announced an intention to step aside after approximately two and a half years with NARA, giving 28 February 2022 as their hire date. They said they would remain during a transfer of NARA assets to the incoming Group Manager and described their Group Manager service as non-permanent by design. The statement also described earlier FEC election-administration work and a role in Public Outreach; these are the author’s own career account.[5]

By mid-August 2024, a transition to AIex as NARA coordinator was underway, and TacticalRummy was moving into a District Court judgeship. The precise completion date of the archival handover remains unspecified.[6]

Chief justiceship and legal reform in 2022

TacticalRummy, serving as Chief Justice RummyLeroux, helped shape the judiciary's response to the 2022 reconstruction. On 14 July he announced preparations for a Federal Bar Association under the Supreme Court. The proposed system combined licensing and ethical regulation with plans for paid legal services and law firms in Boulder County.[7]

He linked the association to a broader path into judicial service. Maintaining lists of qualified attorneys and developing clerkships were intended to make appointments more transparent and less dependent on political competition. Existing practitioners would not have to obtain licenses until fourteen days after publication of the first examination. The announcement remained advance notice, with the directive subject to Supreme Court revision.[8]

The examination he described would have twenty questions across general law, jurisprudence, scenarios and ethics. The plan placed the Federal Bar beneath the Court while maintaining a separate Supreme Court Bar. It therefore returned to professional regulation within the judiciary after the earlier litigation rejecting executive control of attorney licensing.[9][10]

His chief justiceship also intersected with parliamentary reconstruction. In May he attended the State of the Union with associate justices AustinBoBoston1 and 0xSyns; on 28 August he administered oaths to newly elected senators before another State of the Union gathering. In October he announced his retirement effective 30 October and recommended Tony_Giordano as his successor, separating that recommendation from the appointment process itself.[11]
