List of nUSA Presidential Elections with documented ballots and results Presidential Elections in nUSA select the President and, where specified, a Vice President. The individual election articles below distinguish published ballots, electoral certifications and later inauguration announcements. Elections held in the same year are identified by month; the Summer 2016 contest retains its seasonal title because its exact voting month is uncertain.

The list includes 23 contests with a documented winning-ticket announcement or competing result announcements, one additional election with a retrospective winning-candidate record, and three further contests for which only a ballot, a results event or a transition notice is preserved. The presence of an article does not imply that a complete ballot or tally survives.

Reading the figures

Ballots cast, ballots remaining after filtering and votes credited to individual candidates are different measures. Calculated sums are labeled. Candidate entries do not reconcile with reported valid totals in February 2020, March 2024, July and November 2025, and July 2026; the individual articles preserve and explain those differences. November 2024 separately itemizes withdrawn-candidate votes. The March 2026 recount is presented alongside the initial certification.

Campaign polls and debate participants are not treated as proof of a final ballot. Where running mates are absent from a particular record, an omitted name is not treated as proof that no running mate existed.

Additional early election reference

A retrospective NARA entry says Moolah60 was constitutionally elected on 9 June 2013. It does not supply the original ballot or election tally; this reference is not counted among the 23 contests with a preserved result announcement.[50]
