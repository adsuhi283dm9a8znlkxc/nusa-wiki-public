August 2020 nUSA–United Kingdom ultimatum

  • Page
  • Discussion
Appearance
From nUSA Wiki

The August 2020 nUSA–United Kingdom ultimatum was a diplomatic and military threat issued by President BernardCaldwell during his second term in the Roblox nUSA roleplay community. A four-page document dated 6 August 2020 presents a joint ultimatum by nUSA and a Zimbabwe roleplay government. It says a state of war would exist if the United Kingdom group did not accept specified demands by 7 August. A separate two-page congressional document dated 7 August is headed Declaration of War against the United Kingdom, but the copy supplied here contains only introductory text and a link back to the ultimatum. These records establish the threat and a proposed resolution; they do not, on their own, show that the resolution passed or that a later battle occurred.[1]

Background

The ultimatum says that an earlier set of demands had been withdrawn after the exile of a person it calls Finley. It accuses the United Kingdom government of continuing diplomatic insults and threats and says attempts at a diplomatic resolution had failed. These are the nUSA and Zimbabwe signatories' account of the dispute, not an independent record of the United Kingdom's conduct. The document names Prime Minister Phentemjerker and a leader of the Yada Clan among those from whom apologies were sought.[2]

The document is signed in the names of BernardCaldwell, Vice President Lacryma, Secretary of State Federated C. Enfield, White House Chief of Staff Tacus N. Enfield, Principal Deputy Secretary of State Jask M. Enfield, and Savage_Yada, identified there as Zimbabwe's premier and the Yada Clan's leader. The signatures identify who the ultimatum represented as supporting it; the supplied copy does not independently verify each person's consent or role beyond the text of the document.[3]

Demands and proposed war resolution

The ultimatum demanded a public apology to the United States, its citizens and government; a separate apology to Jask M. Enfield; the playing of the United States anthem at parliamentary sessions for three months; United Kingdom support for United States votes in the roleplay United Nations General Assembly and Security Council for three months; and reopening diplomatic channels. It stated that rejection would produce a state of war at midnight British Summer Time on Friday, 7 August 2020. The deadline and threatened consequence are claims made by the ultimatum, not proof of a subsequent declaration or combat.[4]

The second file presents itself as an 81st Congress joint resolution introduced by Teasoup of Florida on 7 August, with BernardCaldwell named as President. Its first page announces a declaration of war, but the text supplied stops after the beginning of Part II. The second page links to a Google Drive copy of the ultimatum, whose content matches the separately supplied four-page file. No vote tally, final enacted text, presidential signature on a completed resolution, or battle report is contained in these two files. Their relationship to any actual hostilities remains to be established from further contemporary records.[5]

Distinction from the April war

The April 2020 nUSA–United Kingdom war occurred under President Reset4K, who publicly described peace talks on 11 April. The August ultimatum is dated almost four months later and names BernardCaldwell as President. Both involve United Kingdom roleplay counterparts, but the reviewed files do not state that the August dispute was a continuation of the April conflict or identify the same negotiated treaty. They are treated here as separate events pending further evidence.[6]
